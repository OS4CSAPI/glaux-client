## 1. Human goal and why both publications matter

The problem is **one source-faithful, useful, reusable Resource Details client for unrelated sensing equipment arrangements**, not a new UI per vendor and not merely standardized but unintelligible CSAPI raw fields. The method is: inspect original equipment and donors fairly → derive bounded model meaning → choose actual, source-backed OGC representation or a separately declared experimental extension → have a producer publish it with source qualifications → have one client discover and interpret it → compare displayed meaning against the original source. An accurate DIM cannot rescue information the integrator omitted; a valid schema alone does not authenticate a false source claim. A local experiment is not a full independent external interoperability result.

**The DIM and Profile are separate publications.** The [new DIM edition](Candidate_Specification/DIM_Profile_V2_Proposed_Research_Candidate_DIM_v0.1_03NOV2026.md) is an implementation-independent **fifteen-proposition R24 candidate** with two diagrams, definitions, evidence hierarchy, four cases, non-forced original donor pedigree, original standard references and validation/limitations annex. Both follow Chicago Manual of Style 18 author–date citations and original linked sources. Step42 did **not** change the old DIM R24 or old PR30 manuscripts or enact their proposals as OGC normative SHALLs.

**Unsettled chassis:** Use one **source-declared focal sensing System/arrangement** with **evidence-qualified typed relations** as the working structural method.
