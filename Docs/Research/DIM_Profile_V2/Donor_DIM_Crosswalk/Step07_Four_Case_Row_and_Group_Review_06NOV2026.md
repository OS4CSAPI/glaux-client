## 1. What this review found, in plain English

The owner-approved horizontal sample is useful because it already permits us to see that **physical sensing equipment is not the same thing as an output field**, and that one product **model** is not one fielded individual. But a table with only 22 rows does not yet describe *the whole sensing arrangement*, particularly **dated equipment service**, **physical mounting**, **power and communications**, **logger/status condition**, **configuration versus default**, and **the path from raw fields to recorded measurements and copies**. The gaps are in the **presently displayed sample**, not proof that none of those meanings existed in earlier R24 or DIM candidate research.

One concrete contrast: Dragino documents **one external SHT31 probe reporting temperature and humidity**; AirGradient documents **two PMS5003 modules at PM1 and PM2 hardware sockets** but has **three PM particulate-output property labels**; Campbell documents **one first-generation ClimaVue 50 assembly with fourteen response positions** and an entirely separate CR1000X Status publisher example containing **31 logger fields**. Conflating units, reported fields, physical components and reporters would erase the manufacturer's own distinctions. None of those three cases proves the configuration or readings of a particular installed unit.

Bennett's Point is different: an **actual station** `acebpmet` has a documented history of **two serial-numbered EE181 probes in three dated service roles**, other individually identified hardware and **two original 2024 NOAA temperature mean events**. **Four older experiment API Observation resources represent those same two originals**, rather than four new observations. The source gives the station location, but **not each probe's surveyed position**; date-precision equipment records also do not identify the exact probe replacement minute. Source-level time/QA and station/logger attribution remain qualified.

The Step07 review is therefore a **pre-review decision register**, not an invitation to quietly populate more donor columns or claim all additional rows are needed in the final model.

## 5. Section-divider review — owner decides at Step08

The **five currently approved navigation rows remain exactly as-is** in the real spreadsheet. As a reading sequence they have strengths: Identity → whole equipment → output/observation → behavior/control → provenance. They are **not** adopted UML packages or classes.

1. **Section 01, Identity & description:** sensible starting point. Later distinguish hardware manufacturer, station operator, source publisher and digital record issuer without folding them into one mandatory identity field.
2. **Section 02, System/equipment/relationships:** needs an explicit **dated installed/service relationship** and **site/mount/physical location** treatment if the model is to explain Bennett's Point beyond an assembly diagram.
3. **Section 03, Channels/outputs/observations:** too much in one area; consider a navigation break between **physical/technical interface**, **field/quantity/schema**, and **actual observation/processing/quality**, preserving each referent and unit.
4. **Section 04, Configuration/control:** defaults and reported settings coexist with commands, but **diagnostics/condition** and **applied configuration** should not be hidden inside generic operating-mode text; actual command acknowledgement/physical effect stages remain untested by these case records.
5. **Section 05, Source/evidence/representation:** the two no-donor proposal rows deliberately remain non-adopted. The Bennett example suggests more than one possible meaning here: original measurement/event identity, original data/QA source, derived attribution, and copies. A presentation break may be needed, not a claim that all are required as DIM classes.

**Suggested navigation principle, not a new mandated eight-module architecture:** retain physical/equipment identity early, make **installation/service and interfaces/outputs** discoverable before **performance/observation and configuration/condition**, and close with **history/provenance/representation**. A final number of groups, names and member rows must follow donor source review and your readability decision, not AI preference.
