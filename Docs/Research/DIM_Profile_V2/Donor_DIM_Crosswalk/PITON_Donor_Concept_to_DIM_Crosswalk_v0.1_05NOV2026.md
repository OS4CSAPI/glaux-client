## 3. Mapping standards and conflict handling

**Each nonempty donor cell** must be grounded in the donor's **real original term, original edition and exact clause/table/schema element**, with a narrow definition. A good mapping says *how and why* meanings overlap and explicitly says *where they do not*. Never infer equivalence based on name alone; note when a “channel” is an addressable transducer service versus a network endpoint or reported data field. Document cell mappings as `DIRECT`, `BROADER`, `NARROWER`, `OVERLAP/PARTIAL`, `RELATED_BUT_DIFFERENT`, `CONFLICT`, `NO_ESTABLISHED_MAPPING`, `UNRESOLVED`; keep the main cell short and the evidence/qualification in a supporting sheet.

**Sources before previous model:** look at the earliest original primary source and its actual publication edition. Project summaries are pointers and interpretations; cite the original OGC, IEEE, OPC, ISA, SAPIENT, AAS, W3C, Open Group, IETF or product/operator issuer for their **own content**. Avoid uploading licensed/proprietary originals. If the original standard is inaccessible, record `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` and the public secondary technical reference reviewed; do not present unavailable clauses as checked.

**Crosswalk behavior:** If the donor expresses a unique concept, give it an independent appropriate row. If its meaning is more specialized or intersects another row, preserve that distinction and potentially split the DIM concept, rather than forcing one mapping. Related elements might require several mapping records. Blank ≠ absent. Avoid a row explosion from every trivial source schema property by comparing relevant *whole-System sensing/equipment* meanings and documenting justified scope exclusions.

**The “least to most used” order** must be recomputed after the substantive comparisons, by **distinct substantively supported DIM rows per donor**, with direct and partial counts separately; no source-count votes, duplicate artifact votes or subjective “best donor” score. Keep the final DIM rightmost no matter how many columns. Fixed donor roster/column orientation is part of the accepted visual design; changing it requires the owner's approval.

**UML diagrams:** ultimately show exact concept **types, attributes, relations and multiplicities only where warranted** by original sources and approved synthesis; must map back to corresponding DIM rows, not independent AI invention. No mandatory top “chassis,” inheritance or modules simply because earlier UML looks attractive.
