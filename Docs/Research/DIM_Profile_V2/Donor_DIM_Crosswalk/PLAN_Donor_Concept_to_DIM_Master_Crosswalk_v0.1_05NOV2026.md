# Donor-to-DIM crosswalk plan

The 40-step source-family and owner-checkpoint sequence is retained below. It is not a 40-prompt promise or proof of complete source coverage. [The PITON](PITON_Donor_Concept_to_DIM_Crosswalk_v0.1_05NOV2026.md) owns current progress, active donor, interrupted delivery and the only next action; [the protocol](../AGENTS.md) governs recovery and execution. Read this plan's applicable row, not all earlier donor reports at every startup.

## Scope and binding controls

Preserve one canonical donor-to-Domain Information Model (DIM) master, the same editable workbook, cases left, donors and grouped Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC) foundation, DIM rightmost, stable row IDs and topic dividers. No silent reordering. Later owner decisions require explicit statuses in completed donor columns; the original sample's blank-cell rule is superseded. Topic dividers and source names do not adopt classes or a chassis.

The 182-artifact register is accountability, not 182 audited donors. Distinguish editions, companion sources, data/semantic models, interface rules and application examples. Compare exact native meanings, retain source grades and caveats, and update existing evidence and mirrors as warranted. Prior candidates and legacy model versions are not accepted by default.

Owner checkpoints remain 08, 16, 24, 34 and 40. Step24 permits decisions on ready coherent portions with meaning, need, relevant alternatives and concrete examples; Steps35–39 reconcile concepts, diagrams and cases; Step40 approves a named version. Do not use donor votes, source counts or class-per-term accumulation as model decisions. No client/server/deployment or device commands are authorized here.

## Delivery controls

One Proceed covers one evidence-sized step. Apply the active donor-specific plan and original-source closure gate, not an old one-row schedule. Later source-first corrections and accepted owner decisions qualify the initial sequence. Preserve manual workbook checkpoints and prior evidence. Report actual source coverage, mapping changes, saved-file parity, checks and one next action; do not infer comprehensive research from a completed plan or workflow.
