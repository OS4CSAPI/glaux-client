# OSUS-R donor review plan

**Decision.** Determine what the pinned OSUS-R implementation contributes to a whole-sensing-system Domain Information Model and a profile-aware Connected Systems client. Its place in the [research order](Donor_Research_Priority_Order.md) follows SAREF Core and Web of Things. All in-scope source meanings may contribute, including distinctions neither earlier donor raised. The owner will decide whether to accept the assessment and, separately, which candidates to adopt.

**Starting evidence.** Use [registered artifact 34 and companions 27 and 59](Step04_182_Artifact_Routing_v0.1_05NOV2026.csv). The [pinned OSUS-R source tree](https://github.com/ssg-udri/OSUS-R/tree/d09088fbe56d4b64f0544d72e98c3cb70fa941f6) identifies the edition. Two verified starting passages distinguish the interface consumers call from the plug-in contract used to reach equipment. The latter also identifies observation fields the core may supply automatically. Trace their definitions before mapping claims. The Klawon thesis and Army Research Laboratory guide provide separately attributed implementation or explanatory context. Record any unavailable original and the exact conclusion that depends on it. **Execution budget.** One further Proceed authorizes the review through verified delivery. The three phases below are internal milestones. Save the source inventory and first findings before integrating the workbook. An interruption resumes at the first unfinished item in the checkpoint.

1. **Read the donor on its own terms.** Inventory the asset-facing interfaces, referenced types, schemas, capabilities and related services at the pinned revision. Identify their native subjects, relationships and source of authority before opening the grid for matching. Cover identity, composition, lifecycle, metadata attribution, observations, capability declarations and other in-scope groups revealed by the inventory. Record stable IDs, paths, symbols or line ranges, source standing and actual reading depth. Follow a dependency when it settles an identified meaning. Group equivalent members with explicit IDs and exceptions. Distinguish an interface requirement, a core-supplied value, plug-in behavior and an example.
2. **Compare in both directions.** For each native group, identify an existing row, a clarification, a genuinely missing distinction, an implementation-specific detail or a specific open question. For every proposed addition, name the nearest existing row and explain what it loses. Then give every baseline information row and each addition an explicit donor disposition. Attach scope and reasoning to negative findings. Check plausible overlaps and over-credit against the originals, including differences in treatment of publisher examples. Test one documented multi-component case such as the ClimaVue sensor and CR1000X logger: which identity or location could a record carry, who could populate it, and what evidence would identify the actual sensing unit? Label the comparison as illustrative unless case evidence establishes deployment.
3. **Integrate, explain and publish.** Begin with the [accepted 493-row workbook](https://github.com/OS4CSAPI/glaux-client/blob/32240618bad639aa71804855e380d6a47866e493/Docs/Research/DIM_Profile_V2/Donor_DIM_Crosswalk/Master_Donor_Concept_to_DIM_Crosswalk.xlsx), checking for later authorized changes. Preserve a browsable pre-change workbook, manual M00/M01, the relative column order, 26 sheets, row-only freeze, dividers, stable IDs and existing evidence. Update the canonical JSON, both CSVs, both readable comparisons and the same editable Excel. Apply the existing color criteria and record explicit prior-donor statuses on new rows. Verify saved values, fills, preserved cells, mirror parity, workbook reopening and the published file fingerprint. Report the actual application tested, material source gaps, reused and new distinctions and implications for the client. Save the result in the existing donor comparison and current checkpoint.

**Finish.** Deliver the verified workbook and source ledger, explain the evidence depth, and stop for owner disposition. A material gap can support bounded-partial delivery. The Step 24 developed-donor adoption checkpoint remains a separate owner decision. Redfish is the next ranked donor after OSUS-R.
