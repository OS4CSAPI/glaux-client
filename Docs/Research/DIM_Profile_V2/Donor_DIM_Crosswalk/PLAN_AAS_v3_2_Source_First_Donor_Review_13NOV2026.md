## Execution and coverage controls

One Proceed is one bounded stage, with splits only when original-source complexity warrants them. Do not invent a prompt quota or repeatedly reopen unchanged evidence. For every relevant native finding, preserve stable ID, issuer/edition, exact locator, element kind, normative standing, actual source-contact grade, qualified relation, caveat, row/candidate/native-only/held disposition and case limits.

Use original meanings and distinct subjects: physical versus logical, kind versus individual, record versus represented asset, declaration versus observation, request versus execution. Generic carriers alone do not establish an application-specific meaning. Unreviewed means no review, not no equivalent. Source-specific serialization rules and examples may remain evidence without new DIM rows. Do not manufacture fields, constraints, equivalences or tests.

Before substantive changes preserve a browsable pre-change checkpoint. Maintain canonical JSON, both CSV and readable mirrors, the same editable workbook, source records and explicit statuses; verify actual saved cells, fills and preserved sheets. Keep M00/M01 immutable and no automatic M02. Record precise lag if any. Four-case ceilings and prior donor holds remain. Actual Microsoft Excel clean reopening is separate from XML, saved-value and rendered checks.

Coverage/accountability, mapping, classification, parity, adoption and runtime conformance are distinct. Key unavailable originals require bounded PARTIAL. O7 recommends an evidence-based disposition for the owner; it does not automatically close AAS. Model adoption remains governed by the broader Step24, Steps35–39 and Step40 checkpoints. No client, server, deployment or physical device operation follows from this plan.
