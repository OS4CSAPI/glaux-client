## Plain-language N1 result (within each donor only)

**Integrated Sensor Architecture (ISA):** Its original 2023 document family employs *multiple organizing schemes*: participating components/controllers and services; the lifecycle stages of their participation; network/presentation/message handling; catalogued services and capability bins; distinct properties/observables/commands; and the separate data types used to carry them. The source organization changes with the purpose of the companion document. The **24 capability bins** mix sensor/application modalities with general, electrical, networking and other concerns; they are **not a taxonomy of twenty-four physical sensor chassis types**.

**The Open Group Sensor Open Systems Architecture (SOSA), C239 Edition 1.1 (2023):** It likewise employs *several native views*: physical system interfaces, functional modules and information flows; logical modules versus implementing infrastructure; conceptual/logical/physical data descriptions; housekeeping/mission task distinctions; mission and support functions; mechanical/electrical hardware profiles; software run-time profiles; and inter-module interactions. Its native module taxonomy is **not an inventory of installed devices**, and parts of its documented module/interface ecosystem remain preliminary, reserved, future, or limited to specialized configurations. This document inventories those source-defined ways of grouping; it does **not yet compare ISA groups to SOSA groups**.
