## 1. Why the audit matters

The goal is a defensible, implementation-independent Domain Information Model (DIM) and a separate OGC API – Connected Systems application Profile that can describe unlike sensing systems. An ISA-native source term can contribute meaning to a candidate DIM information element without being a native exact match, proving real installed equipment or becoming an approved model class. This audit therefore distinguished verified source-native correspondences, deliberately empty unmatched donor cells, and source facts that remain design-review holds.

**31/31 original correspondence targets audited**: 22 proposed DIM information rows and nine case-specific review topics. The 22 break down into **18 qualified ISA source correspondences and four without an established ISA mapping** in this bounded source corpus. The four are a current hosting station relationship, individually addressed transducer channel, a declared general default operating mode, and the project-authored original-source evidence assertion. **No verified match** is not a claim that the ISA family universally lacks an idea. The nine case-specific topics continue as empirical evidence holds, not newly adopted DIM fields.

**25/25 earlier findings audited**: the **11 pre-existing source mapping correspondences** are now source-qualified, including the already corrected non-network-scoped UCI uniqueness wording. The **14 extra source/architecture design findings remain meaningful but intentionally held** for comparison with other donors and later model synthesis. All **six** local original DMS-definition questions were rechecked and remain specifically open. Audited does not mean adopted, implemented, physically verified or authoritatively resolved.
