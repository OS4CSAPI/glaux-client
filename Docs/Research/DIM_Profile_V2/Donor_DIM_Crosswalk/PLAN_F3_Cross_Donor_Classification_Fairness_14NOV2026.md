## 2. One semantics-based decision contract for all four donors

**Source and identity first:** For each relevant source-bearing cell, identify the real native entity and subject (individual physical component, product/type, instance, logical role, software record, design/template, service, installed arrangement or observation), publisher edition, stable original source locator, standing (Rule, Recommendation, Permission, Observation, definition, example, future), and actual reviewed depth (fully read, sampled, unread, inaccessible, irrelevant with reason or unresolved). A source citation in a cell is evidence *to inspect*, not proof that the cell's current meaning or fill is correct.

**Meaning and correspondence next:** Compare the native meaning to the particular existing master row and its qualified case scope. Record EXACT / PARTIAL / BROADER / NARROWER / RELATED / CONFLICT / NO_ESTABLISHED / UNREVIEWED as appropriate. A native source's declaration of an identifier does not prove it identifies a real installed sensing System; a property value is not automatically a performed Observation; a digital operation result is not a verified physical effect. `UNREVIEWED` is never converted into `NO_ESTABLISHED` or donor absence.

**Then propose a candidate classification**, with independent source-based rationale:

| Fill / disposition | Required meaning | Must not be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `COMMON_CANDIDATE` — light blue `#D9EAF8` | A qualified potential information need usable across unlike sensing systems, with original subject and differences retained | Exact cross-donor equivalence, mandatory DIM field or real equipment instance |
| `SPECIALIZED_CANDIDATE` — light amber `#F9E6B8` | A meaningful optional equipment, domain, architecture, industrial or source-specific detail | That every sensing system has it |
| `NATIVE_ONLY_EVIDENCE` — light gray `#E5E7EB` | Retained native API/schema/encoding/packaging or protocol evidence not yet established as a DIM data need | A new universal information field or automatic CSAPI binding |
| `UNRESOLVED_CANDIDATE` — light purple `#E9DEFA` | An explicitly named original-source or mapping question whose potential relevance is unresolved | A convenient substitute for insufficient review or a source-negative equivalence |
| `MIXED_OR_HELD` — **no classification fill** | A composite cell with differing source meanings, unresolved conflict or deliberately qualified no-established comparison; document why, source IDs and next question | That a white cell means the donor has no corresponding concept |
| `NOT_ASSESSED` / `CASE_REVIEW_HOLD` — **no classification fill** | Explicit visible status and exact reason; separate ordinary unassessed rows from nine case review holds | A completed negative audit or evidence of source absence |

The **color describes the potential information need**, not the exactness of a mapping. Different donors with the same potential common need may all be blue **while retaining different native subjects and relation grades**. Conversely, different colors on the same master row must have an explicit original-meaning reason; omission from an older review's row-ID range is **not** such a reason. No donor is favored because its concepts happened to define the early master rows.
