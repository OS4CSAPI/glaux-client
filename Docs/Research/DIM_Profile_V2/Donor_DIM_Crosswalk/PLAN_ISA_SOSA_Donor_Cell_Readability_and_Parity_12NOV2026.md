## Purpose and exact problem

The approved master is intended to compare *meaning* horizontally: four equipment/source cases left, donor columns next, related sensing/observation foundations together, proposed DIM rightmost. Each donor cell should be readable without losing source meanings, negative equivalences, source-edition qualifications, or the ability to find its original evidence. ISA and The Open Group SOSA currently have materially different cell-writing conventions. The discrepancy does **not** establish relative donor importance and the column ordering remains provisional pending the established cross-donor ranking at Steps 35–39.

**Verified baseline at research HEAD `4f303fd` (12 November 2026), canonical CSV/Excel G3-N2 snapshot:** 329 information rows, 15 columns, 15 topic dividers (346×15 saved grid), same original editable 26-sheet workbook, 22 historical sheets preserved. ISA column: **11,057 characters, 1,559 words, 316 nonempty cells, longest 165 characters**. SOSA column: **107,985 characters, 14,535 words, all 329 cells populated, longest 3,889 characters, 61 cells over 500 characters, 37 over 1,000**. SOSA has **211 cells containing exactly `Not assessed here`**, **31 additional cells that start `Not assessed here` but append source findings**, and **87 other source-bearing cells**. These categories total 329. Counts are *presentation diagnostics*, not source-completeness or ranking measures.

The accumulated SOSA G2/G3 packets often appended a separate, fully caveated note into the same comparison cell. ISA mostly stores concise semantic summaries with detailed evidence elsewhere. A SOSA cell may contain useful, distinct original-source findings; length alone does not make it inaccurate. Some mixed `Not assessed here` labels are potentially misleading after later qualified source contact. Conversely, terse ISA text may need a better source pointer or qualification. This plan addresses both, not just character-count reduction.

## Binding method / approved cell contract (apply only within a separately authorized step)

1. **Concise comparison, never a research-log dump:** Give the native donor concept(s), relationship to the *existing row topic*, critical scope or negative-equivalence caveat, and a **stable existing source locator/evidence reference**. Where distinct original meanings deserve separate treatments, preserve them explicitly; do not collapse multiple relation grades into an invented exact match. Use a few precise phrases/sentences; a loose ~200–400-character preference is **not a hard cap**.
2. **Preserve source qualifications:** Clearly separate 2023 C239 from authentic 2022 Edition 1.0 DSDM/IDL evidence and limited 2025 S251 metadata. Retain Rule/Recommendation/Permission/Observation/Future versus descriptive design/example and installed/current/runtime distinctions. Do not manufacture 2023 schema equivalences, CSAPI support, physical installation, commands, authorizations, or Profile SHALLs.
3. **Triage assessment states per row:** `Not assessed here` means genuinely unassessed; `NO_ESTABLISHED` means reviewed evidence does not establish that particular correspondence; `RELATED`, `PARTIAL`, `NARROWER`, etc. retain their original scope. Especially reconcile **31 mixed-prefix cells individually** from original packet ledgers. Do not globally replace all 211 plain placeholders, infer absence from missing entries, or count a source pointer as a full clause/companion audit.
4. **Keep every original claim recoverable:** Before shortening a cell, locate all materially distinct claims and their identifiers/edition/caveats in already committed ISA/SOSA source ledgers, decision records or prior committed cell text; preserve their provenance in the existing evidence. If a needed claim exists **only** in the verbose cell and cannot be traced safely, retain/mark a hold or attach a narrow traceability correction to the **existing** ledger; never silently delete the only evidence or create a fabricated source. Git commit history preserves the complete pre-edit cell text. No new standalone ledgers/workflows/scripts by default.
5. **Do not transform the master structure:** Same 329 topics, 15 columns and divider order; unchanged four source cases, other donor columns, OGC block, proposed DIM column, row IDs, ISA 480 reviews/six original questions, 776 qualified SOSA packet-to-row contributions/117 distinct affected rows, 1,583 packet source-ID references, nine grouped SOSA source holds, and all existing DIM/Profile non-adoption. Only source-qualified **ISA/SOSA cell presentation** and directly necessary existing evidence/status pointers may change. A true semantic contradiction is a **hold requiring owner direction**, not license to rewrite source interpretations.

**Illustrative target, not a replacement decision:** A physical-constituent cell might read: “SOSA Hardware Element/PIC is a specialized physical constituent; logical Module and software RTE are not automatically installed devices [C239 §§4–5, G2-2/G2-7T; 2022 DSDM G3-N2 RELATED]. Does not establish independently exposed CSAPI Systems.” All detailed chassis, connector, profile, conditional-rule and edition findings remain in their original packet evidence, not erased. Actual wording is determined row-by-row from existing source records during an authorized step.
