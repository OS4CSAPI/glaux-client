## 1. Plain-English result: three identities, two meanings of "type", and physical source caution

**A physical or logical asset is not its AAS digital shell.** IDTA defines an asset widely enough to cover equipment, software and intangible organizational assets, and defines AAS as a standardized *digital representation*. This is explicit in original [terms and definitions lines 44–83](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/terms-definitions-and-abbreviations.adoc#L44-L83) (stable source IDs **AAS02-N001/N002**). The shell has its own globally identifiable ID inherited from `Identifiable`; the represented asset may have `globalAssetId` and/or `specificAssetId` under separate source rules. An idShort is a *local model name*, not the globally unique shell ID or automatically a sensor serial (N006, N009, N012, N018–N021).

**The source uses two different type/instance axes.** `AssetInformation.assetKind` has **Type, Instance, Batch, Role, NotApplicable**. Separately, `HasKind.kind` used by submodels denotes **Template or Instance**, with Instance as its default. A manufacturer product kind, a particular physical probe, a submodel template and a populated submodel are not the same object. Original [core asset-kinds and IDs](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/spec-metamodel/core.adoc#L69-L120) and [AssetKind enumerations](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/spec-metamodel/core.adoc#L166-L198) differ from [HasKind/ModellingKind](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/spec-metamodel/common.adoc#L296-L352) (N008/N011/N016/N017). The [informative type/instance annex](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/annex/general.adoc#L118-L195) explicitly says an instance asset need not have a corresponding type asset and distinguishes AAS templates from asset types (N034–N036/N042).

**A model relationship does not establish physical attachment.** `AssetAdministrationShell.derivedFrom` (0..1) refers to another **shell** from which it is derived; `submodel` (0..*) refers to information describing an aspect of the asset. A source `Reference` has distinct external/model targets and ordered keys, and even an identifier spelled like a URL need not be fetchable. `RelationshipElement` represents first/second references with semantics, and `Entity` can describe parts of a composite asset or bill of materials; neither proves a real attached cable, operational parent, deployed sensor or current field configuration without original instance evidence. See source [AAS core](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/spec-metamodel/core.adoc#L20-L53), [references](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/spec-metamodel/referencing.adoc#L14-L151), [RelationshipElement](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/spec-metamodel/submodel-elements.adoc#L740-L769), and [Entity](https://github.com/admin-shell-io/aas-specs-metamodel/blob/ecd191273a28dd9c4e7bb75e923f0f0fe5da444e/documentation/IDTA-01001/modules/ROOT/pages/spec-metamodel/submodel-elements.adoc#L400-L432).
