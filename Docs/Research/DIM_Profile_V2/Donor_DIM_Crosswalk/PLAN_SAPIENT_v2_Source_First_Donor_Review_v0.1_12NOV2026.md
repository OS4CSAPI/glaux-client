## 1. What we are trying to learn, in ordinary language

The Domain Information Model (DIM) and separate OGC API – Connected Systems (CSAPI) Profile should eventually help one common client explain **which actual sensing system and equipment are involved, how they relate, what the system declares it can do, what it reports or does, and what evidence supports those facts**. We have examined the Integrated Sensor Architecture (ISA) and The Open Group Sensor Open Systems Architecture (SOSA) at bounded source-first depth and accepted a comparison of their native organizational schemes. SAPIENT v2 may reveal different information boundaries: interface **Nodes**, declared capabilities and modes, changing status, reports, task requests, acknowledgments, and the meaning of intermediary/fusion roles.

**Do not assume SAPIENT is the universal chassis or the hierarchy for the DIM.** This is a source-first assessment of its own original interface scope, not a vote on which donor wins. The work must be useful for comparing donor meanings while preserving the separate, independent standing of CSAPI and other sources.

## 3. Source issues already on hold — investigate, never guess away

The prior original-source challenge documents six exact limitation families (see its §5). Retain their IDs and determine the source-qualified disposition, not a desired answer.

1. **`SAP-ISSUE-01`:** BSI Registration Table 15 labels `config_data` as field 9; pinned `registration.proto` has `reporting_region=9` and `config_data=10`. Different source bytes/field identities must not be silently reconciled.
2. **`SAP-ISSUE-02`:** BSI's outer timestamp describes message **content** time, whereas pinned protobuf comments describe message **sent** time. Neither implies OGC observation time.
3. **`SAP-ISSUE-03`:** protobuf optional/repeated syntax is not the same as BSI mandatory/desired/conditional (M/D/C) applicability.
4. **`SAP-ISSUE-04`:** middleware routing, caching, replay and validation options are **implementation-specific**, not normative source guarantees.
5. **`SAP-ISSUE-05`:** test-harness limitations and lack of executed conformance cannot be treated as passing device interoperability.
6. **`SAP-ISSUE-06`:** no native, source-declared SAPIENT Node→CSAPI System, Task→Command or Detection→OGC Observation equivalence was established in the reviewed scope.

Also protect source-specific distinctions already noticed: `Node`/sender UUID versus physical sensor serial and individual system identity; manufacturer `model` versus `NodeType`; recursive `sub_components` versus installed physical parts; `dependent_nodes` versus physical containment; `ModeDefinition` versus selected/current mode; `StatusDefinition` versus actual `StatusReport`; `Task` versus `TaskAck` versus actual physical effect; detection/event content versus original observed act; and reported power percentage versus electrical voltage.

## 4. Approved ISA rigor reused as a method, not as an artificial iteration count

**Start with originals.** Enumerate BSI's exact document sections, tables, annexes, labelled requirements, examples and normative references; all **16** pinned proto files, message/enum/field groups, code options and associated comments; and the limited scope of the middleware, harness and guidance. For each relevant source item/section, retain stable IDs, original edition/locator, what was actually read, source kind (Rule/Recommendation/Permission/Observation/descriptive definition/example/future as applicable), source authority and precise holds. Distinguish **fully read / sampled / pointer-only / unread / inaccessible / irrelevant with reason / unresolved**, and count a heading or filename only as inventory unless contents are substantively checked.

**Reuse evidence without inventing coverage.** Map previous `S2-N##` and `SAP-ISSUE-##` to their original source clauses and protobuf lines, verify source standing, and mark what earlier work covered or did not. A source reference or prior AI claim is a search locator, not proof of a full clause/field audit. If original bytes or companion editions cannot be authenticated, leave named holds rather than substitute another release.

**Compare meaning, not words.** Source-to-master relationships may be EXACT, PARTIAL, BROADER, NARROWER, RELATED, CONFLICT, NO_ESTABLISHED or UNREVIEWED, with explicit negative-equivalence caveats. A SAPIENT Node can be a software/fusion/platform role and may not equal an individual physical sensor; an acknowledged request does not prove hardware effect. Source-native normative requirements do not become CSAPI Profile SHALL statements or DIM fields by accumulation.

**Progressively integrate the one master.** At each source-qualified substantive increment, update canonical `Master_Donor_Concept_to_DIM_Crosswalk.json`, **both** CSV mirrors, **both** GitHub-readable Markdown comparisons, and relevant existing source/coverage evidence. Preserve four case columns on the left, donor columns, grouped OGC foundations, proposed DIM at far right, topic dividers, prior donor data, source IDs and original status/holds. **Do not create new classes or add one row for every proto field.** Update the **SAME original editable 26-sheet Excel** at substantive checkpoints; if it temporarily trails the canonical texts, state exact older saved snapshot and changed-cell count in the PITON/status. Verify saved workbook cells and sheet preservation at each Excel checkpoint, not just command success or a checksum.

**No redundant busywork.** One specific source-sized increment per owner `Proceed`, with original source findings and a concise human explanation. One meaningful publisher plus independent saved-file checker when Excel is refreshed; no duplicate reruns for purely administrative changes. Update the *existing* PITON/plan/status/reusable lessons only when warranted. Keep one compact SAPIENT source/section coverage record and one progressive donor-source findings record as necessary, rather than proliferating per-message documents or creating another master workbook.
