## Plain-English research findings

Registration is a *statement about a network participant*. It identifies a Node, declares its interface version, capabilities, modes, expected status reports, and configuration information. A registered Node may be a fusion, platform, human-associated or effector participant, so Node identity/NodeType must not be converted automatically into a specific individual hardware sensor or general DIM class.

Declared capabilities, acceptable operational modes, a default mode, future reporting fields, heartbeat interval and task definitions are different information functions. They do **not** prove that a mode was selected, that a position or electrical level was measured, that an alert occurred, or that a physical control action succeeded. BSI §6.2.2 separately requires Registration before other messages, distinguishes accepted/rejected RegistrationAck from malformed-message Error, and specifies reconnect/retry behavior after a 30-second unanswered attempt. These requirements belong to the SAPIENT messaging protocol, not automatically to ISA or a CSAPI Profile.

ConfigurationData can recursively describe subcomponents, but the first element must be the interfacing component and software-only Nodes may use the serial-number slot for a product/license number. The data structure cannot validate physical part-of, electrical connection, installed asset identity or vendor-equipment relationships without additional evidence. Registered LocationType selects the coordinate system/datum used later, not the equipment's current geographic position.

## Source conflicts and holds

**Prior `SAP-ISSUE-01` CONFIRMED, still open:** BSI Table15 lists `config_data` as tag **9**, while pinned `registration.proto` declares `reporting_region=9` and `config_data=10`. The BSI table does not list the field-9 reporting_region member. Neither standard/document is silently rewritten. All other earlier six SAPIENT issue families and SAP-02's five newer exact holds persist.

- **SAP03A-H01:** BSI §6.2.5.5/Table21 gives Duration.value tag2, but pinned Dstl registration.proto reserves2 and declares value=3. No field-wire repair.
- **SAP03A-H02:** BSI §6.2.5.10/Table27 describes maximum_latency as a retry-communication interval, while pinned registration.proto comment describes detection-to-message latency. No assumed equivalent.
- **SAP03A-H03:** BSI §6.2.5.13/Table30 UNSPECIFIED=0 implies no association in its description; NONE=1 and the pinned enum distinguish no association from unspecified. Apply no silent value-equivalence.

The original Table20 LocationType allows field-dependent coordinate formats; its `location_definition` excludes RangeBearing even though the reusable protobuf LocationType permits such an option in other contexts. The original Table26 `Node_Stopped/Node_Paused` meanings follow task state rather than proving a platform is physically stationary. A native `ModeType` omission/default convention also is not demonstrated to be the same as protobuf zero-valued UNSPECIFIED. These are scoped interpretive cautions; they are not a new invented universal rule.
