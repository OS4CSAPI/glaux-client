## 1. Owner-readable conclusion: what the four donor challenges actually changed

**The five original candidate DIM distinctions survived their source-qualified challenges. None was disproven in its *narrow original meaning*. But F-03 (method/capability versus performed activity), F-04 (composition/component standing), F-02 (type/model) and F-05 (identity/digital representation) are not sufficiently detailed for a useful, faithful *whole-System* model as currently written.** It is important not to use “survived” as a synonym for “finished.”

The four challenger families provide **different, complementary answers** to important questions:

- **SAPIENT v2:** a Node's *declared abilities and possible operating modes*, *currently reported mode and status*, *requested Task*, *Task acknowledgement/status* and *reported Detection* are **different information claims**. This matters to commands and the user's operational view. See [S2-N02–N10](02_SAPIENT_Challenge.md#3-direct-source-owned-observations-with-precise-locators), §§6.2–6.5 in BSI Flex 335 v2 and pinned official Protocol Buffer schemas.
- **IEEE 1451/ISO 2145x:** a **physical transducer**, the **TIM/NCAP interface roles**, an **addressed transducer channel**, its **TEDS self-description**, and **runtime measurements** have different referents. IEEE 1451 offers **substantial accessible 2024-linked TEDS technical evidence** (Meta TEDS, Transducer Channel TEDS, optional calibration/frequency-response/transfer-function/units/time metadata) from the [IEEE Playground and NIST material already recovered in Step 03 §0](03_IEEE1451_Challenge.md#01-verified-accessible-public-2024-linked-evidence--omitted-from-the-original-step-03); it is **NOT technically unavailable as a DIM donor**. Exact uninspected IEEE 1451.0-2024 normative field requiredness/cardinality still needs authoritative text.
- **ISA 6.0:** ISA Composition's **physical/internal membership**, its **CIC-mediated external visibility** (opaque, visible, virtual), and **taskability** (transparent vs translucent) are different properties. Its CCD **declares Property/Observable/Command capabilities** while Status/Event/Request/Response communications realize other information roles. See source-qualified [ISA04-S01–S05](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md#3-source-native-findings-most-relevant-to-the-candidate-model), ISA SDD 6.0r5 §§3, 4.2.2 and 5.1.1; ISA DMS 6.0r12 §2.
- **The Open Group SOSA:** the **whole Sensor** may contain distributed hardware and software, yet remain distinct from **HostPlatform**; the edition-specific DSDM differentiates **functional Component_, Module_, manufactured Product and ProductInstance, SensorComponent part-of, HostPlatform and ModuleImplementation**. **System Manager versus Task Manager**, **health versus faults/BIT**, and **conceptual/logical/physical representations** are further source-native distinctions. See [SOSA04-S01–S07](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md#3-source-native-findings-most-relevant-to-the-candidate-model), SOSA Edition 1.1 C239 §§4–6 and Edition 1.0 DSDM §§1.1.3.15, .54, .68–69, .82–83, .89–92.

**The synthesis is a better-described network of roles and relationships—not a ranked winner and not seven mandatory new classes.** A donor can be structurally valuable for a *subtopic* even when its own root or native API architecture is broader/narrower than the provisional individual equipment-realized sensing System viewpoint. That directly repairs the donor-selection bias identified after the first twelve-step plan.

## 5. Deduplicated new meaning questions — seven source flags are not seven classes

The Step 02 SAPIENT report recorded **two** `SAP-NC` candidates; corrected Step 03 IEEE report recorded **two** `IE03-NC` candidates; Step 04 ISA/SOSA report recorded **three** `DC04-NC` candidates. There are **seven original source-derived candidate flags**, with overlaps. They are editorially grouped into **five research seams** below rather than counted as seven independent standards confirmations or seven DIM modules. **No candidate is adopted; all mapping and transformation proof remains OPEN.**

| Cluster | Which original issue IDs are preserved | The unanswered *meaning* question (not a class proposal) | Tentative home in the five-topic manuscript |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **DC05-G01 — declaration, allowed behavior, current state** | `SAP-NC-01`; `DC04-NC-03` | How to distinguish declared capability/reporting contract, allowed operating mode, selected/current configuration, current mode, readiness and status **without assuming each is the same Procedure or activity**? | F-03 primarily; touches F-02/F-05. |
| **DC05-G02 — request, acknowledgement, actual result, tests** | `SAP-NC-02`; `DC04-NC-03` | How to distinguish allowable command, requested task, acceptance, failure/completion, detection/Observation, diagnostic test and BIT result **without claiming they necessarily imply each other**? | F-03 primarily; condition/control remain future subjects. |
| **DC05-G03 — addressed channel and qualified self-description** | `IE03-NC-01`; `IE03-NC-02` | What is an **addressable sensing/actuation channel**, which physical element/module/functional property does it describe, and which *units, ranges, uncertainty, calibration, sampling, timing and performance* apply at that particular scope? | F-02/F-04/F-05; **IEEE Playground evidence is usable** now. |
| **DC05-G04 — physical, functional, product, host topology** | `DC04-NC-02` | How does one describe *the same System* through physical assembly, functional roles, products/implementation, hosting/deployment and managed subcomponents **without asserting an unsupported single hierarchy**? | F-01/F-02/F-04. |
| **DC05-G05 — outward visibility and authority of intermediaries** | `DC04-NC-01` | Which physical/functional member exists, which appears externally as virtual/opaque/visible, what is independently taskable, and **whose information and identity** does the intermediary actually expose? | F-04/F-05; provenance/time across all subjects. |

**One issue may support more than one cluster** (e.g. `DC04-NC-03` in G01 and G02). The clusters are an **editorial grouping for later model prose**, not a claim that the donor texts converge onto five formal ontology classes, that one framework is the universal base, or that the task/condition work has been performed. Separate source-specific richness must be retained even when a common qualification is proposed.
