## 1. Introduction, problem and intended use

### 1.1 Purpose

The proposed DIM is intended to express the shared **meaning** needed to understand equipment-realized sensing Systems as operational subjects, independently of the manufacturer, application programming interface (API), encoding, database, or user interface that describes them. Its motivation is a practical interoperability problem: two different sensing-equipment products may address similar tasks yet communicate their identities, detector or transducer arrangements, supported functions, available choices, selected settings, reported conditions and lifecycle information using different structures and labels. Merely exposing their descriptions through a common service may preserve data without giving an unfamiliar client enough conventions to interpret that data appropriately; conversely, a source-to-standard conversion may have omitted the meaning in the first place. A DIM can help specify what a representation is *about*, what different assertions mean, and what distinctions should survive an integration. **[CANDIDATE synthesis; purpose controlled by the [owner's problem baseline](../DIM_Profile_V2_Problem_and_Outcome_Baseline_v0.1_28OCT2026.md).]**

The original TRSS and OmniSense examples made the intended result concrete: one consistent, intelligible System Resource Details experience that preserves the differences actually found in each vendor's equipment rather than replacing every vendor interface with an identical list of generic fields. The later Smart Citizen Kit 2.3 research supplies a separately documented third type of evidence: product design, assembly, sensor-chip/measurement relationships, firmware, and vendor software object definitions. These sources can expose errors in interpretation before a Profile is imposed, but they have **not** established any authenticated individual Smart Citizen Kit, an operational source-to-CSAPI transformation or lossless client display. The common UI is **an evaluation goal**, not a DIM class or an implemented behavior ([Problem/Outcome Baseline](../DIM_Profile_V2_Problem_and_Outcome_Baseline_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), §§1–4; [I13 integrated case report](../Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I13_Integrated_Smart_Citizen_Case_Study_and_Gate_D_v0.1_28OCT2026.md)). **[DOCUMENTED project purpose and bounded evidence; no runtime test.]**

An implementation-independent information model describes a problem domain's entities, semantics and relationships rather than prescribing one lower-level data representation; multiple data models or interface bindings may realize its meanings (Pras and Schönwälder 2003, §§2–4). For this project the intended sequence remains **donor/source meanings → defensible DIM → selected CSAPI application Profile → producer representation → profile-aware client**. The DIM is the conceptual reference; any later Profile would make selected meanings predictable and testable in particular CSAPI/SensorML representations without claiming that every donor standard has been implemented. This division follows the existing [V2 conceptual method](../DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md), §§2, 8–9, and [publication outlines](../DIM_Profile_V2_Provisional_Document_Outlines_v0.1_26OCT2026.md), §§3–5. **[CANDIDATE method; not a completed Profile.]**

### 1.2 Working subject boundary—description is not root-class adoption

**[CANDIDATE / OPEN]** The current research describes **individually distinguishable, equipment-realized Systems whose defining operational role is sensing or observing**, considered as whole Systems rather than solely as measurement producers. Such equipment may also communicate, compute, store information, supply or consume power, control other devices, maintain configurations, or participate in deployments; these functions do not require every internal constituent to itself be a Sensor. The published OGC API – Connected Systems (CSAPI) Part 1 model uses distinct classification facets such as `systemType = Sensor` and `assetType = Equipment` (OGC 23-001 v1.0, §9.2.2). They are **source-native facets for the intended interface**, not a newly established compound DIM class or the evidence for a universal superclass ([boundary research](../DIM_Profile_V2_Emerging_Subject_Boundary_Pedigree_v0.2_26OCT2026.md); [Step 04](04_Individual_System_Identity_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), `ALN-T01-A010-01`).

This is a *working universe of discourse*, not yet a final domain name, closed system taxonomy, mandated root, or exhaustive statement about all actors and simulated systems. In particular, **scope** (which real-world subjects the DIM is intended to describe), **model root** (which source-supported class might someday anchor a formal schema), and **Profile target** (which service/resource/encoding must satisfy eventual requirements) are three separate choices. Terms such as “physical sensing systems” and “Sensor + Equipment” remain explanatory shorthand while donor-supported classification and scope are evaluated; they are not adopted standards-defined family names. The older Managed Physical Sensing Systems terminology and V1 eight information subjects remain **predecessor evidence**, not controlling definitions or preassigned V2 packages ([concept method](../DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md), §§4, 6–8; [architecture blueprint](../DIM_Profile_V2_Provisional_Architecture_Blueprint_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), §§2–4).

### 1.3 Explicit boundary of this first foundation

This manuscript addresses **five investigated semantic distinctions only**: (F-01) an individual System as subject, (F-02) reusable kind/model descriptions, (F-03) methods versus actual performance, (F-04) physical and functional component relationships and independent standing, and (F-05) subject versus digital representation/proxy identity. Their role is to keep source meanings coherent and distinguishable, not to declare five UML classes or a complete common core. Command behavior, performance/capability metrology, active configuration, deployments over time, power, communications, condition/recovery, sampling/location, security and full observation/result modeling are **additional whole-system information needs** requiring separately justified analysis. Their mention here is contextual; **none is claimed as a completed concept family, class, constraint or future module** ([provisional blueprint](../DIM_Profile_V2_Provisional_Architecture_Blueprint_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), §3).

The previously promoted *Anatomy Phase B/C/D* architecture remains **quarantined**. Its old labels are not evidence of class adoption. The completed Smart Citizen I01–I13 case study's conditional approval concerns its written source-research report **only**, not verified physical-to-digital interoperability or a complete DIM ([case study README](../Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/README.md); [I12 critical challenge](../Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I12_Critical_Interoperability_Challenge_v0.1_28OCT2026.md)). **[EXCLUSION and evidence ceiling.]**


## 2. Standards foundation and interpretation method

### 2.1 Source pedigree and relative authority

This foundation reuses named meanings from several established standards rather than inventing a replacement supermodel. CSAPI Parts 1–2 and SensorML 3.0 supply relevant API/system-description carriers (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, 2025b, 2025c); OMS 3.0 supplies observation/observer/procedure concepts (Open Geospatial Consortium 2023); the 2017 Semantic Sensor Network Ontology and its **21 October 2026 Working Draft successor** require **distinct edition-specific references** (World Wide Web Consortium 2017; World Wide Web Consortium and Open Geospatial Consortium 2026). SAREF Core 4.1.1, AAS Part 1 v3.2, OPC UA Part 100 Devices v1.05.0 and W3C WoT Architecture 1.1 contribute different viewpoints on individual devices, product kinds, components and digital services (ETSI 2024; Industrial Digital Twin Association 2026; OPC Foundation 2025; World Wide Web Consortium 2023a). The pinned OSUS-R Asset/AssetProxy Java sources are **implementation evidence only**, not a general donor-authored ontology (OSUS-R Contributors 2023, source commit `d09088f`).

The investigated population comprises **182 inventoried *source artifacts***, not 182 distinct standards, 182 independent donor votes or 182 completed semantic maps ([Step 01 corpus baseline](../Donor_Alignment/01_Corpus_Baseline_and_Exceptions.md); [Step 03 source-accountability register](../Donor_Alignment/03_Source_Accountability_Register.csv)). Steps 04–08 created **48 topic-scoped alignment records involving 12 frozen source IDs**. The [Step 09 cross-concept reconciliation](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md), §3, groups fifteen topic-local source-declaration records into ten scoped evidence bundles; repeated dependencies are not counted as independent corroboration. **Source-native meaning and cited edition control a fact**, a qualified research alignment controls only its narrow proposed mapping, and **neither automatically adopts a DIM concept or proves conformant implementation**.

The 2026 SOSA/SSN Working Draft includes **non-normative** inter-ontology and selected CSAPI correspondence tables. Published CSAPI 1.0 names the **2017** SSN Recommendation as a reference. The API tables' labels `sosa:implements`, `sosa:hasSubSystem` and `ssn:hasSystemKind` are not automatically verified RDF properties in the cited 2017 Recommendation, which defines `ssn:implements` and `ssn:hasSubSystem` in the checked text. **[OPEN: edition-sensitive URI-level relationship; do not silently substitute predicates.]** See [Step 09 ISSUE-R09-01](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md) and the original [CSAPI §§9.2.2 and 10.3](https://docs.ogc.org/is/23-001/23-001.html), [2017 SSN](https://www.w3.org/TR/2017/REC-vocab-ssn-20171019/) and [dated 2026 Working Draft](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/WD-vocab-ssn-2023-20261021/).

### 2.2 How a source meaning becomes *candidate* DIM text

An existing source provides its own term, definition, relationship scope, cardinality and conformance status. A V2 alignment records whether a selected meaning is *close with qualifications*, *partly overlapping*, *broader/narrower*, *unresolved*, *rejected*, or only *design inspiration*; the comparison is directional and does not replace the source's concept. Candidate DIM prose then selects the **shared semantic distinction actually supported**, states important non-equivalences, and leaves undecided any relation, taxonomy, cardinality, representation or requirement not established by evidence. The chain retains exact source identifiers and the difference between **donor-declared dependency** and **researcher-inferred conceptual fit** ([Step 02 register method](../Donor_Alignment/02_Register_Method_and_Schema.md), §§2–5; [Step 09](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md), §§2–4).

The **preferred reuse method** is “reuse invariants; specialize variation.” A recognized common distinction can be described once, but product-specific terms and genuine differences in physical realization, installation, permitted choices, output granularity and state cannot be erased just to create a uniform taxonomy. An eventual module/subdomain would require evidence of internal semantic cohesion, bounded dependencies and reuse; neither the donor classification bins, V1 subjects nor headings in this manuscript are modules. The project-specific [modeling convention](../DIM_Profile_V2_Modeling_Convention_v0.1_26OCT2026.md), §§2–4, permits UML **only when source-backed classes and associations warrant a meaningful view**. **No UML diagram is introduced in this version**, because the current evidence primarily establishes roles and non-equivalences, not a decided root, class ownership, multiplicity or specialization hierarchy.

## 3. Conceptual overview: one subject, several non-interchangeable descriptions

**[CANDIDATE overview]** The meaningful object of inquiry is a particular sensing-oriented equipment System. A description of its kind may characterize multiple individuals without identifying this unit; descriptions of the methods it could follow are different again. A historical Observation or act, when supported by actual data, records what happened under particular circumstances, not simply what was documented or possible. The subject may have integral or temporary physical associates, process components and outputs, yet its *part relationships* do not determine which internal elements ought to be independently identifiable Systems. Finally, information resources, software database records, proxies and services may mediate access to that subject while retaining their own identity, validity, time and role. This connected reading is supported as a **set of distinctions**, not as one formally asserted graph or an accepted class schema ([Step 09 crosswalk](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md), R09-01–R09-12).

To make the relation picture explicit without implying a UML diagram:

> An **individual equipment System** can have source-qualified **kind/model descriptions** and can be described as **capable of implementing reusable methods**. Its actual **observations/activities and results**, if available, are separate occurrences with their own method, performer and time evidence. Its constituents and external associations have **different physical, functional, lifecycle and representation roles**; its digital descriptions and interfaces are **not the equipment**. A correct integration preserves the meanings it can substantiate and records the limits of what it cannot. **[V2 research synthesis, CANDIDATE; no named ontology properties or multiplicities adopted.]**

The following sections develop these meanings as **manuscript prose**, rather than renaming them five new UML classes. The entry labels `F-01`–`F-05` are editorial devices for traceability only.


## 4. Candidate concept meanings and their donor-native boundaries

### 4.1 F-01 — Individual sensing-equipment System as subject

**[CANDIDATE DIM wording.]** An **individual System** is a particular real-world, equipment-realized subject to which information about sensing-related purpose, realization, configuration, operation, associations and observations may refer. Its identity as a subject is conceptually distinct from the representation of that subject in an API or database, its product name and kind, its identifiers, and any observation or activity associated with it. A source may identify a System without describing all its internals; conversely, a detailed product description can exist without identifying an actual installed unit. This meaning concerns the *referent* of a description rather than a new class called `IndividualSystem`. **[SUPPORTED_FOR_CANDIDATE_DIM for the distinction; no adopted class.]**

**[SOURCE-NATIVE pedigree.]** CSAPI Part 1 describes individual `System` resources and differentiates resource identity from a real-world `uniqueIdentifier` (OGC 23-001, §§8.4–8.5, 9.2.2). SAREF's device and device-kind distinction (ETSI 2024, “Device kinds and devices”), AAS's Asset and Shell/AssetInformation identities (IDTA 01001 v3.2), and OPC UA Device versus AddressSpace/node representation (OPC 10000-100, §§3–6) supply related but **not equivalent** perspectives. The 2017 SSN Recommendation's `ssn:System` and the 2026 Working Draft's `sosa:System` are different **edition-specific** source terms (W3C 2017, §4.9; W3C/OGC 2026, §§5.3.5, 8.16). The directly sourced comparisons, including one **deferred Open Group SOSA** item, are in [Step 04](04_Individual_System_Identity_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), `ALN-T01-A010-01`, `ALN-T01-A020-01`, `ALN-T01-A019-01`, `ALN-T01-A144-01`, `ALN-T01-A097-01`, `ALN-T01-A104-01` and `ALN-T01-A049-01`.

**[OPEN / exclusion.]** No universal identifier syntax, physical replacement-continuity rule, one-resource-to-one-equipment mapping or exact UML superclass is established here. A matching software record ID, station page ID, MAC address, authentication token, configuration filename or product model is not independent evidence that two representations refer to the *same* physical System. The future Profile must distinguish inherited CSAPI requirements from any new restrictions; the DIM does not turn CSAPI's recommended long-lived System UID into a universal identity theorem ([Step 04 ISSUE-T01-02](04_Individual_System_Identity_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md); [Step 09 R09-01 and ISSUE-R09-07](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md)).

#### [v0.2 DONOR CHALLENGE] F-01 — whole equipment, managed participant and identity of the described subject

A **bounded operational sensing subject** and its **interface participants** are not automatically the same individual. The Open Group SOSA Edition 1.1 §4.1.1.1–.3 treats a whole `Sensor` as a System spanning one or more hardware elements, sometimes distributed on a distinct Host Platform. That source-native Sensor scope is unusually relevant to the project’s provisional whole-equipment question; its defense-domain sensor categories are **not adopted as a universal class**, however. [The Open Group source review](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md#3-source-native-findings-most-relevant-to-the-candidate-model), `SOSA04-S01/S02`, identifies the original Edition 1.1 clauses and separate DSDM relationships. This strengthens the **whole subject** reading without settling the universal chassis, model root or naming question.

SAPIENT v2 `Node/node_id` identifies a message/interface participant (including fusion/platform/software roles), the ISA 6.0 `Component` may be a software interface participant or virtualized composition member, and IEEE 1451 `APP/NCAP/TIM` distinguish application, mediator and transducer-interface roles. Those roles may concern or expose particular equipment **only if source evidence links them**; sender UUID, virtual member ID, management resource ID and serial number are not universal physical-identity synonyms. [SAPIENT S2-N01](02_SAPIENT_Challenge.md#3-direct-source-owned-observations-with-precise-locators); [ISA04-S01–S03](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md#3-source-native-findings-most-relevant-to-the-candidate-model); [IEEE IE03-S01–S02 and corrected §0](03_IEEE1451_Challenge.md#0-corrective-evidence-finding--ieee-1451-is-technically-usable-as-a-dim-donor-28-october-2026). **[CANDIDATE qualification; physical referent equivalence and UID continuity OPEN; no new `System` subclass.]**

### 4.2 F-02 — Reusable kind or model description, qualified by source meaning

**[CANDIDATE DIM wording.]** A **reusable kind/model description role** characterizes a kind of System, manufactured product, or otherwise shared specification that may be relevant to multiple particular Systems. Such information may include manufacturer-model designation, common technical characteristics, allowed choices and documented capabilities; it need not establish the configuration, installed options, calibration, status or measurement history of any specific individual. Information described as *type* or *model* may instead be an ontology classification, process inheritance target, AddressSpace ObjectType, or network-interaction template, and the **nature of that source role must remain visible**. The role is not itself proof that all sources support one interchangeable or separately identified DIM `ProductModel` class. **[CANDIDATE meaning, no universal type instance cardinality.]**

**[SOURCE-NATIVE pedigree.]** CSAPI `System.systemKind` references a `Procedure` description of the System kind/datasheet, while SensorML 3.0 `typeOf` relates specific/general processes and their configuration patterns (OGC 23-001, §§9.2.2, 13.1; OGC 23-000, §§7.2, 8.2.9.3). SAREF 4.1.1 uses `DeviceKind` and `hasDeviceKind`, while AAS v3.2 distinguishes Type and Instance Assets. OPC UA Part 100 separates a manufacturer's `Model` nameplate property from the information-model `DeviceType`, and WoT's `Thing Model` is primarily a reusable *interaction* template (ETSI 2024; IDTA 2026; OPC Foundation 2025, §§4.5.2, 4.7; W3C 2023a, §§6.1.1.2, 7.1). Those source-defined meanings are compared in [Step 05](05_Product_Model_Kind_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), `ALN-T02-A010-01` through `ALN-T02-A132-01`; **OPC UA DeviceType = manufacturer product model** is an explicitly **[REJECTED EQUIVALENCE]**, not a broad rejection of OPC UA.

**[OPEN / exclusion.]** No universal kind URI, versioning, inheritance semantics, multiplicity or installation policy has been selected. A manufacturer model-name string may not link to a resolvable technical datasheet. Product-design alternatives are not evidence of installed hardware; a nominal capability is not an actual recorded state. AAS `AssetInformation.assetType` (type Asset reference) is specifically **not** the same property meaning as CSAPI `assetType = Equipment` (classification of realization). The WoT Thing Description 1.1 specification examined as companion evidence is **outside** the frozen 182-source inventory; see [Step 05 ISSUE-T02-01](05_Product_Model_Kind_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md).

#### [v0.2 DONOR CHALLENGE] F-02 — what a description applies to, not merely whether it says “type”

A **manufacturer product designation**, **source-native modality/architectural role**, **supported sensing function**, **reusable interaction template**, **installed option**, and **channel-scoped technical datasheet** can describe related equipment while answering different questions. SAPIENT v2 `NodeDefinition.node_type` (sensing or architecture role) differs from `ConfigurationData.model` (reported manufacturer model), while ISA `Component Capability Description` declares Properties/Observables/Commands rather than establishing a particular manufactured product family. In The Open Group SOSA Edition 1.0 DSDM, `ProductInstance` specifically associates a manufactured `Product` with a functional `Component_`; that association does not make Product, function and individual assembled System one object. [SAPIENT S2-N02](02_SAPIENT_Challenge.md); [ISA04-S04](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md); [SOSA04-S04](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md).

IEEE 1451 provides substantial, *presently usable* evidence for descriptions at **module, transducer-channel and calibration/performance scope**. The 2024-linked [IEEE 1451 Playground Meta TEDS](https://iml.ubi.pt/playground/docs/ieee1451/teds/meta-teds/), [Transducer Channel TEDS](https://iml.ubi.pt/playground/docs/ieee1451/teds/transducer-channel-teds/) and [Optional TEDS](https://iml.ubi.pt/playground/docs/ieee1451/teds/optional-teds/) expose technical description examples including **physical units, channel type, range/uncertainty, sampling/timing, calibration and frequency response**. They are meaningful donor concepts, **not inaccessible merely because the licensed full normative text is not present**; only exact unverified mandatory/conditional fields and conformance obligations remain **SOURCE_ACCESS_LIMITED**. A TEDS containing type-like characteristics and a unit-specific calibration cannot automatically be a whole-product kind description or proof of current installed hardware. [Corrected IEEE Step 03 §0](03_IEEE1451_Challenge.md#0-corrective-evidence-finding--ieee-1451-is-technically-usable-as-a-dim-donor-28-october-2026); [reconciliation DC05-G03](05_Challenge_Reconciliation.md#5-deduplicated-new-meaning-questions--seven-source-flags-are-not-seven-classes). **[CANDIDATE described-subject and applicability distinction; no universal ProductModel/ChannelDescription class, URI or cardinality.]**

### 4.3 F-03 — Procedure/Method, implementability, execution and result

**[CANDIDATE DIM wording.]** A **reusable Procedure or Method** describes a way of performing a task, processing inputs or determining a property value. That description is different from a particular System capable of carrying it out, and both are different from an actual occasion on which a Procedure was used and a result was produced. An individual System may be described as *implementing* a Procedure; the claim of implementability alone does not assert a dated Observation, completed command, executed method version or produced result. A method may be documented as a protocol, algorithm, software reference, process-flow structure or part of broader equipment documentation, and its descriptive level depends on the source. **[CANDIDATE meaning; no new mandatory Method or Execution class.]**

**[SOURCE-NATIVE pedigree.]** Published CSAPI Part 1 uses the `Procedure` resource type for **both** a System-kind/datasheet description and a method that a System can implement, but differentiates their **association roles**—`systemKind` versus `procedures` (OGC 23-001, §§9.2.2, 13.1–13.2). CSAPI Part 2 separately associates procedures with a `DataStream` and optionally an individual `Observation`, with observation-level parameters and results (OGC 23-002, §§9.2.2, 9.7). SensorML `ProcessMethod` can describe a SimpleProcess method; aggregate process structure can convey dataflow without a stand-alone method object for every physical process (OGC 23-000, §§8.3–8.4). OMS 3.0 relates Observer, ObservingProcedure, Observation and result, while the published 2017 SSN and 2026 Working Draft express related implement/use distinctions with **different property editions** (OGC 20-082r4, §§8.2, 8.5–8.6; W3C 2017, §4.8; W3C/OGC 2026, §§5.3.3–5.3.5). The exact source/relationship qualifications appear in [Step 06](06_Procedure_Method_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), `ALN-T03-A010-01` through `ALN-T03-A019-02`.

**[OPEN / exclusion.]** OMS's conceptual Observation cardinality for an observing procedure is **OMS-native**, not an adopted DIM-wide or CSAPI Profile multiplicity: the CSAPI Part 2 Observation/stream procedure references are optional. Step 06 used accessible official OMS excerpts but **could not re-fetch the entire large official HTML document**; normative reuse of its cardinalities needs a targeted full-text recheck ([Step 06 ISSUE-T03-03](06_Procedure_Method_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md)). The 2026 Working Draft's `sosa:ObservingProcedure` remains **non-normative/pending implementation experience**. No method reference, firmware function or command may be elevated to proof that a particular physical unit performed an Observation without executed source data and provenance ([Step 09 ISSUE-R09-02/03](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md)).

#### [v0.2 DONOR CHALLENGE] F-03 — declaration, active state, requested action and actual outcome

The original **reusable Method versus performed occurrence** distinction remains sound but **does not by itself cover operational meaning**. A System’s **declared capability** is not the same assertion as its **permitted modes and reporting contract**; those differ again from its **selected configuration**, **current mode**, **reported readiness/condition**, **requested Task/Command**, **acknowledgement**, **execution outcome** and **observed result**. In SAPIENT v2, `Registration` declares capabilities and `ModeDefinition` options, `StatusReport.mode` reports the current mode, `Task` is a request and `TaskAck` distinguishes accepted/rejected/completed/failed. A `DetectionReport` has distinct report/object identities and is not automatically the consequence of a successful Task. [BSI Flex 335 v2 §§6.2–6.5, source-qualified Step 02, S2-N03–S2-N07](02_SAPIENT_Challenge.md#3-direct-source-owned-observations-with-precise-locators). The official companion protobufs are encoding evidence, **not** a normative equivalence to CSAPI `Procedure/Command/Observation`. The published BSI `Registration.config_data` versus pinned `registration.proto` field-number disagreement remains **OPEN**.

ISA 6.0 separates **Component Capability Description** for Properties, Observables and Commands from Status/Event/Request/Response messages and readiness/reporting states (SDD §§4–5.1; DMS §2). The Open Group SOSA distinguishes **System Manager** (keeping equipment configured, healthy and supported) from **Task Manager** (mission execution) and separates defined built-in tests from performed BIT results (Edition 1.1 §§4.2, 6; Edition 1.0 DSDM §§1.1.3.89–92). The equipment’s *reported source status* must carry appropriate time, scope and provenance and must not silently become a current measured state or health verdict. **[CANDIDATE v0.2 expansion; F-03 materially incomplete in v0.1; actual runtime mapping OPEN / NO_TRANSFORMATION_TEST.]**

### 4.4 F-04 — Composition, contextual associations and level of separate description

**[CANDIDATE DIM wording.]** An individual System may be understood as a whole and through meaningful constituent elements, some of which may themselves qualify as independently described Systems. **The relationship to the whole**—for example integral membership, non-permanent association, operational hosting, deployment, communication or functional/dataflow connection—is not the same question as **the level of independent description or management** given to that element. An internal board or sensor chip can be real, important and separately described while lacking evidence of an independent physical System identity or CSAPI System resource. A measured channel, processing step or software association likewise does not by its name or output alone denote a separately installed item of hardware. **[CANDIDATE two-axis distinction; no universal component superclass or whole–part property.]**

**[SOURCE-NATIVE pedigree.]** CSAPI Part 1 §§10.1–10.3 permits independently described System subsystems and distinguishes **Composition** (integral/ordinarily enduring) from **Aggregation** (non-permanent association via Deployment). The same standard's subsystem endpoint may also expose **currently deployed** subsystems (§10.5), while a parent may recursively expose descendants' streams (§10.7). The producing System is established by the relevant CSAPI Part 2 `DataStream.system` relation, not merely by the parent's navigation association (OGC 23-001, §§10.2, 10.5, 10.7; OGC 23-002, §9.2.2). SensorML `PhysicalComponent`/`PhysicalSystem` and `AggregateProcess` express **process description granularity**, which need not match a complete hardware component inventory (OGC 23-000, §§8.4–8.6). The 2017 SSN's `ssn:hasSubSystem` and the 2026 Working Draft's `sosa:hasSubSystem`, hosting and Deployment relations have separate namespace/edition status; OPC UA Devices and SAREF `consistsOf` cover additional physical/software and broader composite meanings (W3C 2017; W3C/OGC 2026; OPC Foundation 2025, §§4, 9; ETSI 2024). The detailed directional comparisons are in [Step 07](07_Composition_Component_Standing_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), `ALN-T04-A010-01` through `ALN-T04-A144-01`.

**[OPEN / exclusion.]** The evidence does not define a compulsory test for which sensor board, transducer, bus channel, antenna, storage module or relay becomes a standalone System resource. A module's cable, removable connector or listing under a nested endpoint does not itself prove either an enduring or temporary lifecycle association. The source-specific CSAPI use of the English word *Composition* must not be imported as the stricter lifecycle semantics of a UML composite-aggregation diamond. Differences in hosting, mission role, deployment period and network connections need their own evidence; they are not synonyms of physical ownership. See [Step 07 §4](07_Composition_Component_Standing_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) and [Step 09 R09-06–R09-08](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md).

#### [v0.2 DONOR CHALLENGE] F-04 — multiple relationship dimensions, not one equipment tree

The original distinction between **what is physically part of a whole** and **what deserves independent identity/description** is necessary but not sufficient. The Open Group SOSA makes it possible to analyze **whole Sensor versus SensorComponent part-of**, **functional Component_/Module_**, **manufactured Product/ProductInstance**, **module implementation**, **hosted-on HostPlatform**, **physical Chassis/Slot relationships** and **indirect management** as **different native relations** (Edition 1.1 §§4–6; separate Edition 1.0 DSDM §§1.1.3.15, .54, .68–69, .82–83, .92). The DSDM's native association cardinalities belong to **that 2022 source edition**, not automatically the 2023 architecture or the broader DIM. [Step 04 SOSA04-S01–S05](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md).

ISA `Composition` exposes internally existing members through a CIC that can keep them **opaque**, represent them **virtually**, make them **visible**, or permit/withhold **external taskability** (transparent versus translucent). The **network’s participant/resource hierarchy** can therefore differ from the equipment's **real physical member list**, and source-origin attribution can be mediated. SAPIENT `ConfigurationData.sub_components`, platform `dependent_nodes` and Fusion Node hierarchy are respectively configuration self-description, operational dependency and message organization, **not three names for permanent physical ownership**. [ISA SDD 6.0r5 §4.2.2 and Fig. 2; Step 04 ISA04-S02/S03](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md); [SAPIENT Step 02 S2-N08/N09](02_SAPIENT_Challenge.md).

IEEE 1451's addressable `TransducerChannel` and TIM/submodule scope provide a separate **functional access/measurement** dimension, distinct from a physical chip, measured property or CSAPI `DataStream`. The [2024-linked Playground Transducer Channel TEDS](https://iml.ubi.pt/playground/docs/ieee1451/teds/transducer-channel-teds/) gives **technically rich, current-edition-related descriptions**; this **does not prove** a particular Smart Citizen SHT31 chip implements IEEE 1451 or has one channel per output. [Corrected IEEE Step 03 §0](03_IEEE1451_Challenge.md); [Step 05 DC05-G03–G05](05_Challenge_Reconciliation.md#5-deduplicated-new-meaning-questions--seven-source-flags-are-not-seven-classes).

Accordingly, a **candidate source-independent description must qualify at least the *kind of relationship*** when known: physical membership, functional implementation, manufactured-product realization, source-described channel, deployment/hosting, operational dependency, network communication, mediated visibility/taskability and independent managed-resource standing. This is a **list of distinguishable perspectives**, not a new nine-property DIM ontology, a mandatory package structure or a flat part inventory. A physical attachment cable or nested API resource cannot itself establish permanence. **[CANDIDATE relational expansion; F-04 materially incomplete in v0.1; no universal `hasComponent` or UML composition assertion.]**

### 4.5 F-05 — Equipment, digital description, proxy, service and their identity roles

**[CANDIDATE DIM wording.]** The equipment System that a source describes can be distinguished from the resource, digital information model, database entry, document, intermediary or service through which its information is published or accessed. A representation may describe one equipment individual, a product type, a function/aspect, a virtual subject or a group, depending on its source-defined purpose. The **identity of a description or service** is not necessarily the **identity of the equipment**; equal, unequal or changing identifiers do not establish a physical-to-digital equivalence without evidence of what they identify and the conditions under which the relationship holds. Information obtained from a proxy, offline engineering record, simulation or cached source may also have different authority and time meaning from current equipment observations. **[CANDIDATE distinction; no DigitalRepresentation, DigitalTwin or representedBy class/property adopted.]**

**[SOURCE-NATIVE pedigree.]** CSAPI distinguishes the System's URI-valued `uniqueIdentifier` from the server-local resource URL/ID and acknowledges multiple services describing one subject (OGC 23-001, §8.5). AAS distinguishes an Asset from its separately identifiable Administration Shell and aspect-specific Submodels, with **one represented Asset per Shell as an AAS-local condition** (IDTA 01001 v3.2). WoT Architecture distinguishes physical/virtual Thing, Thing Description, Intermediary and Digital Twin; an intermediary may preserve or assign a new proxy Thing ID and a twin may represent more than one Thing (W3C 2023a, §§5, 6.10.2, 9.2.2). OPC UA Part 100 separates device nameplate information from AddressSpace Nodes and its optional online/offline models, with distinct NodeIds for paired parameters (§§4.5, 6.1–6.3); SAREF's Service can digitally represent a **function**, not necessarily a whole Device (ETSI 2024). The pinned OSUS-R `Asset`/`AssetProxy` interfaces distinguish a consumer-facing software façade from a device-specific plug-in **as implementation evidence, not formal ontology** (OSUS-R Contributors 2023). See [Step 08](08_Digital_Representation_Proxy_Identity_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), `ALN-T05-A010-01` through `ALN-T05-A034-01`.

**[OPEN / exclusion.]** The current sources do not support a universal same-ID rule, one-resource-to-one-physical-object cardinality, digital-twin superclass, proxy protocol or cross-vendor alias identity test. WoT Thing Description 1.1 is an **official out-of-corpus companion**, not another frozen artifact; its identifier rules must not replace CSAPI's. AAS's one-Asset-per-Shell relation cannot impose a uniform rule on WoT, OPC UA or V2. No Smart Citizen platform `Device` or `Component` has been proven to denote a physically authenticated SCK2.3 unit or one specific chip. Future profile/client decisions must distinguish authoritative, configured, stale and derived values only when the source provides that information ([Step 08 ISSUE-T05-01–04](08_Digital_Representation_Proxy_Identity_Alignment_v0.1_28OCT2026.md); [Step 09 ISSUE-R09-07–09](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md)).


#### [v0.2 DONOR CHALLENGE] F-05 — whose representation, whose status, which time?

A description/resource can refer to a **physical whole**, **subcomponent**, **channel**, **function**, **product kind**, **virtual participant** or **group**. A SAPIENT `node_id` belongs to the source participant; a routed/archived Apex message can have **originating sender, destination, message content time, mediation, and possibly replay/caching** semantics. An ISA opaque member’s capabilities may be exposed under the CIC identity, while a visible virtual member may be assigned its own interface ID; the true physical member count or reporter cannot be reconstructed from network resource counts alone. A SOSA System Manager may indirectly manage a hardware element, while its DIM `ProductInstance` and `SensorComponent` relations describe different referents. IEEE `NCAP/TIM/TEDS` separates the interface participant, its digital self-description's scope and the underlying transducer source. [Step 02 S2-N01/S2-N10](02_SAPIENT_Challenge.md); [Step 04 ISA04-S02/S03, SOSA04-S04/S06](04_Other_Structural_Challenges.md); [corrected Step 03](03_IEEE1451_Challenge.md).

A faithful representation may need **(a) what subject the statement is about; (b) who originated/mediated the assertion; (c) whether the value is permitted/designed, configured/selected, actually reported, derived/estimated or directly measured; and (d) the time and source version under which the claim holds**. These are **candidate interpretation obligations**, not proof every source provides all these values, a universal field schema, one-to-one aliases or current-state authority. SAPIENT BSI v2 message content time versus companion protobuf send-time commentary remains a specific **OPEN** binding issue, not resolved by this prose. The source never makes cached data a fresh observation merely because a client loaded it. [Step 05 DC05-S07–S10](05_Challenge_Reconciliation.md#4-cross-donor-seams-what-really-corresponds-and-what-does-not). **[CANDIDATE F-05 qualification; no universal digital-twin class, guaranteed alias relation or event-time rule.]**

### 5.1 Non-conflation conditions supported by the evidence

**[CANDIDATE interpretive integrity.]** The repeated source findings justify preserving the *difference between roles* even when a later encoding legitimately uses one resource for several purposes. A kind description and an implementable method are different CSAPI associations despite sharing a `Procedure` target type; available manufacturer hardware options and actual installed configuration are different assertions; an observed result is not a description of supported output alone; a physically attached constituent is not necessarily a full managed subsystem; and one source software “Component” is not necessarily an installed component. These are semantic *guardrails* for later model review, **not additional DIM or Profile obligations**. Trace: [Step 09](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md), R09-02–R09-12.

**[CANDIDATE evidentiary integrity.]** In later mappings, an absent optional relation should be distinguished from a positively established absence of equipment, method, event or capability. Statements about product designs, possible options, specific physical individuals, runtime software responses, sensor observations and derived estimates belong to different evidence standpoints; they should not be silently promoted from a less-specific source. Information about a value may additionally need units, qualifiers, timestamps, calculation/origin and relevant version before it can be faithfully interpreted. The **detailed modeling of these qualification concepts is still open**; the present manuscript records why they matter without making unsupported attribute declarations.

### 5.2 Constraints and multiplicities deliberately *not* generalized

The normative/structural constraints of a **particular donor model** may matter in its own binding while not being intrinsic truths about this broader domain. Examples are CSAPI's required System UID, optional `systemKind` and `procedures` links, OMS's Observation-method cardinality, AAS's one represented Asset per Shell, optional WoT TD identifier, and distinct OPC UA NodeIds for online/offline Parameter Nodes. These do **not** jointly imply one unconditional cross-standard multiplicity, identifier scheme or universal class partition (OGC 23-001, §§8–13; OGC 20-082r4, §8; IDTA 01001 v3.2; W3C TD 1.1; OPC 10000-100, §6.3). **[OPEN: intrinsic constraints versus future binding-specific requirements.]** Any eventual conformance text would require a selected target, exact edition, dependency analysis and test evidence; none is authored here.

## 6. Common meaning, variation and future specialization

The strongest candidate common meanings identified so far concern **the referent of a description and its qualified roles**. They are useful across multiple manufacturing, ontology and API families precisely because an instance, its type/kind, its supported method, its actual activity, its constituents, and its digital representations are not interchangeable. This does not imply that each distinction should be implemented as a discrete class. A donor may package kind and method in one document; a fielded System may combine sensors and computing internally; another source may identify components and deployments separately; and a service may provide a single function or aggregate several Things. A future conceptual design can reuse the stable distinctions and **specialize only where source-native roles truly differ** ([method basis](../DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md), §8; [Step 09 reconciliation](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md), §2). **[CANDIDATE strategy, no module decisions.]**

Whole-system information beyond the five investigated topics remains essential to the overall objective. Previous work recorded identity/technical description, equipment/physical arrangement, sensing/performance/outputs, deployment, configuration/modes/actions, communications, power and condition/service/lifecycle as **V1 information subjects**. They may eventually reveal reusable modules, cross-cutting qualification concepts, specializations or separate source adapters, but that cannot be decided from list headings alone. In particular, a TRSS detector-slot configuration and OmniSense transducer inventory should not be flattened into identical source structures merely because both describe sensing. Their distinct histories, command patterns and power/condition reports also should not be forced into the five candidate topics without further evidence ([Problem/Outcome Baseline](../DIM_Profile_V2_Problem_and_Outcome_Baseline_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), §3; [provisional blueprint](../DIM_Profile_V2_Provisional_Architecture_Blueprint_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), §§3–5). **[OPEN: actual core/module/specialization architecture.]**

The **five-part foundation is intentionally incomplete**. It can serve as a defensible *initial portion* of a model manuscript because its claims have been constrained, sourced and reconciled; it cannot claim to describe the whole system, all common domains, all relevant donor commitments, or the particular completeness needed for a publishable Profile. Progress to a true common foundation will require investigating the other necessary whole-system meanings without losing source compatibility or manufacturing example-specific structure. The order and authorization for any later investigation are outside Step 10.

### 7.2 Important directly observed *non-equivalences*

The most instructive information is often what **must not** be inferred. CSAPI v1.0's use of `Procedure` in two association roles does not make product kind and executed method synonymous. CSAPI `assetType` and AAS `AssetInformation.assetType` have different native meanings, and OPC UA's `DeviceType` is not a manufacturer's product model just because it contains “Type.” WoT proxy identity can diverge from the originating Thing, while AAS Shell identity differs from Asset identity under AAS-specific relationships. A recursive CSAPI parent–datastream association does not itself identify the parent as the actual producer. SAREF `consistsOf` can describe composite properties and values as well as equipment, and SensorML process-component links do not necessarily describe hardware assembly. These exclusions are supported by [Steps 05–09](../Donor_Alignment/09_Cross_Concept_Reconciliation.md) and their original donor references.

### 7.3 Case evidence as *falsification*, not an adopted DIM instance

The Smart Citizen manufacturer documentation describes a Kit 2.3 design with distinct Data Board and Urban Board, an ordinary **in-enclosure cabled particulate-matter sensor**, and chips such as **SHT31** (temperature and humidity) and **AS7331** (several ultraviolet channels). The pinned backend application, however, defines a software `Component` as a **Device–Sensor-definition association**, not a separately authenticated mounted IC ([manufacturer assembly](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/guides/getting-started/kit-assembly.md#L121-L147); [sensor table](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/boards/urban-board.md#L47-L59); [pinned backend Component](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-api/blob/25a524cbfb036f8950c247b847d8884a4e776297/app/models/component.rb#L1-L7)). The product source supports these **design and software meaning distinctions** but does not prove which chips, optional sensors or firmware versions are installed in a particular fielded Kit. It likewise does not establish a physical-to-API record join, actual Observation, reliable mapping of multiple channels to actual DataStreams, or provenance of a real result ([I07](../Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I07_Composition_and_Component_Standing_Mapping_v0.1_28OCT2026.md); [I08](../Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I08_Digital_Representation_and_Five_Pass_Reconciliation_v0.1_28OCT2026.md); [I12](../Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I12_Critical_Interoperability_Challenge_v0.1_28OCT2026.md)).

The earlier source/standard comparison rejected four **naive mapping rules**, including treating documentation of supported measurements as actual observation-derived DataStream metadata and treating a parent's recursively visible streams as directly produced by that parent. **Those were hypothetical interpretation errors, not observed failures of a running integration**. The verdict is **conditional source-grounded semantic coherence and useful failure prevention**, not a validated source-to-DIM-to-Profile-to-client exchange. Our original TRSS/OmniSense equipment and wireframe examples remain essential for a later multi-vendor test, but their historical or synthetic fixtures are not adopted equipment-instance data in this manuscript ([I12](../Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I12_Critical_Interoperability_Challenge_v0.1_28OCT2026.md); [Problem/Outcome Baseline](../DIM_Profile_V2_Problem_and_Outcome_Baseline_v0.1_28OCT2026.md), §3). **[LIMITED empirical status.]**
