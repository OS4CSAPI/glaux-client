**Purpose:** Give the previously under-mapped **SAPIENT** lineage a real source-by-source opportunity to challenge the five existing candidate meanings **F-01–F-05**, without assuming that a networking Node must replace the working physical sensing-equipment subject or that a named SAPIENT field automatically becomes a V2 class.

## 1. In plain language: the research result

**SAPIENT v2 substantially strengthens the operational side of what we may eventually need the DIM to describe.** The actual standard distinguishes: a participating Node and its source identity; the modality/types and capabilities a Node **declares** in Registration; the **modes** and task forms it says it supports; its configurable/subcomponent descriptions and dependencies; its **reported current mode/status**; a **Task request**; a separate **Task acknowledgement** with ACCEPTED/REJECTED/COMPLETED/FAILED outcomes; and detected/alerted events reported as distinct messages. This is more precise than referring generically to “a method a System can implement” whenever we mean configuration, ability, current operating state, or executed activity.

The old candidate meaning F-03, **reusable Method versus actual occurrence**, **survives**; but it is **insufficient on its own** to capture SAPIENT's explicitly differentiated capability, mode, task, response/status and result roles. Thus there are **NEW_MEANING_CANDIDATE** findings for later synthesis: **(a) declared operational/measurement contract versus selected/current state and performed outcome**, and **(b) task/command request versus its separately recorded acknowledgement and outcome**. This is a *challenge to completeness*, **not evidence that we need two new mandatory DIM classes**, that these are equivalent to CSAPI Procedure/Command resources, or that real vendor integrations already expose every one of these facts.

SAPIENT also **supports our earlier cautions** about physical identity and composition. Its `Node` is an information/interface participant, with node roles ranging from sensing equipment to fusion, mobile/pointable platform and (in its `NodeType` enumeration) human participation. A registered `node_id` is an interface-level message sender UUID; `ConfigurationData.serial_number` may identify physical equipment **or** a software product/license depending on the Node. Recursive `sub_components` describes configuration data, **not automatically a hierarchy of authenticated physical CSAPI Systems**, and `dependent_nodes` represents operational/tasking effects, **not necessarily part-of or hardware ownership**.

**Most consequential source-check finding:** the official BSI Flex 335 v2.0 PDF and frozen official Dstl v2 protobuf disagree about the **top-level Registration field serial**: **BSI Table 15 labels `config_data` as field 9**, whereas pinned `registration.proto` uses **field 9 for `reporting_region` and field 10 for `config_data`**. Annex A of the PDF itself lists `reporting_region` among the v2 protobuf additions. This is an **OPEN documentation/protobuf binding discrepancy**, not a reason to invent a different message schema or an automatic normative fix. Do **not** put its disputed serial number into a future CSAPI Profile requirement without a separately justified resolution.

## 4. The actual five-topic DIM challenge and explicit dispositions

**Interpretation key:** `UNCHANGED` means the earlier *distinction* is still defensible; `QUALIFY` means SAPIENT supplies a material scope/role refinement; `CONTRADICT` would require a *specific conflict* with an earlier candidate statement, not merely different vocabulary; `NEW_MEANING_CANDIDATE` means previously underdeveloped distinct semantics needing later study; `OPEN` means the source-to-DIM mapping cannot yet be asserted. These are **V2 ANALYST decisions**, not BSI-authored equivalence declarations. **All adoption statuses remain NOT_ADOPTED; NO_TRANSFORMATION_TEST.**

## 6. Relevance to actual TRSS/OmniSense client behavior

The resulting practical questions for a *future*, profile-aware shared System Details view are better than “Which tab do we use?”:

- **TRSS detector options:** Distinguish *supports seismic/acoustic at slot 1*, *slot 1 is configured as seismic/acoustic in a supplied historical record*, *SAPIENT Node declares a sensing modality*, *ModeDefinition advertises behavior* and *StatusReport says which mode is actually active*. These are **not five interchangeable sources of installed hardware truth**.
- **OmniSense reported transducer labels:** Do not convert a reported list into five SAPIENT Node UUIDs or independently deployed CSAPI System UIDs. `sub_components` and dependent Nodes might add descriptions but require native source and real identity/association evidence.
- **Operations and control:** A UI needs to display *what actions are supported*, *what Task was requested*, *whether a node accepted it*, and *whether the outcome is completed, rejected or failed*. It must not replace this lifecycle with a single green “command succeeded” derived from the existence of a TaskAck.
- **Power and diagnostics:** SAPIENT's status Power level is a **reported percentage** with its own message semantics; it is not equivalent to TRSS's estimator-based 100% remaining-capacity output or OmniSense Core5ADS's 15.43 V DC and six separate diagnostic Pass findings.
- **Time and provenance:** A Node ID, object ID, report ID, source/content timestamp, software/proxy cache and actual fielded unit are different evidence roles. Without a verified join and field history, a client should label those honestly rather than generate physical IDs or live observations.
