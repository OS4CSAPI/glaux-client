## 1. What the selection screen found

The previous 48 detailed mappings used **nine** donor lineages positively and **one** further lineage only in a deferred record. **Twenty** of the 30 previously extracted lineages did not receive detailed concept-by-concept comparison in those records. This does **not** mean we lacked preliminary research on them. SAPIENT, IEEE 1451, ISA and The Open Group SOSA deserve a fair opportunity to challenge our *five candidate meanings* before we adopt architecture or proceed to additional subjects.

The old eligibility error was to give donors too little weight because their source type (for example an interface specification) or broad system-of-systems scope did not define our entire chassis. **A source can make a vital contribution to one DIM meaning without being its root.** This Step 01 gives each lineage an evidence-qualified routing decision, **not** a donor ranking or claim of equivalence.

## 2. How to read the complete matrix

A P or — **never means not applicable to the eventual whole DIM**.

Existing triage **G = GREEN** (earlier usable structural extraction, **not normative certification**) and **Y = YELLOW** (specific access/authority restrictions, **not rejection**). The “prior” column is only detailed Step 04–08 alignment coverage. Each source-section link goes to the existing extraction's GitHub source-line locator, **not** a normative clause.

## 3. The four focused challenges selected

**Step 02: SAPIENT v2 (C12; frozen BSI primary ID 21, Dstl companions 22–25).** Node identity, Registration, kinds, capabilities, modes, operational status, `ConfigurationData.sub_components` and dependencies, Tasks/Acknowledgements and software middleware offer evidence across all five meanings. We must read v2's original definitions and pinned schema; **not** presume v3.

**Step 03: IEEE 1451/ISO 2145x (C03; primary IDs 110, 114, 124).** NCAP/TIM/TransducerChannel/TEDS and self-description challenge whether our physical chip, channel, measurement, model and digital interface meanings are precise enough. The exact IEEE 1451.0-2024 normative TEDS fields, obligations and cardinalities remained **access-gated**; no invented text.

**Step 04A: Integrated Sensor Architecture (C04; primary IDs 37, 42)** — Component/Composition, interface/virtual participant, capability declaration and controller/registration challenge our whole/part/managed-resource assumptions. **Step 04B: The Open Group SOSA (C15; primary IDs 48, 49)** — whole Sensor, Module/ProductInstance, HostPlatform and management/condition challenge the physical equipment view. **This is distinct from W3C/OGC SOSA/SSN**, and the previous F01 ID 49 was only deferred. These are **two selected challengers**, not winners.

**Why other candidates are deferred rather than discarded:** Redfish and RFC 8348 will be strong when investigating physical inventory/condition, PA-DIM ID 103 remains a legitimate specialization absent from the 48 records, LwM2M and Matter address device-management and endpoint structures, MIMOSA and EMAN address lifecycle/health/power, I-ADOPT and OMS address measured-variable/observation specifics, and PROV/NIEM can strengthen provenance and reuse practices. Each has an explicit route in the matrix. Keeping Step 04 at two families prevents a new unbounded review.
