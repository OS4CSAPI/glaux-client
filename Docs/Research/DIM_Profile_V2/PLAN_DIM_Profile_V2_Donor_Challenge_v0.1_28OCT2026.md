## 1. Why we need this short challenge

The preceding effort produced **five studied candidate semantic distinctions** and [the first candidate DIM manuscript](Donor_Alignment/10_Candidate_DIM_Foundation.md), based on **48 source-concept mapping records from 12 frozen artifact IDs** (including one deferred). The detailed donors were mainly CSAPI, SensorML, SOSA/SSN, OMS, SAREF, AAS, OPC UA Devices, WoT, and bounded OSUS-R implementation evidence.

But our earlier [full-corpus chassis challenge](DIM_Profile_V2_Chassis_Corpus_Wide_Reconciliation_v0.1_25OCT2026.md) and [30-lineage source extraction](DRAFT_DIM_Profile_V2_Anatomy_PhaseA_Independent_Extractions_v0.1_26OCT2026.md) had already recognized other serious structural contributors. **BSI Flex 335 v2.0 / SAPIENT** is a source-native Node, registration, capability, operating-mode, configuration, status, hierarchy and tasking model. **IEEE 1451 / ISO 2145x** is a smart-transducer, channel and electronic self-description lineage, albeit with incomplete current normative-text access. Both matter for exactly the kinds of System meanings we're seeking to model. **Integrated Sensor Architecture (ISA)**, **The Open Group SOSA**, **OPC UA PA-DIM**, **Redfish/CIM**, and other extracted lineages can also challenge particular concepts. The prior phase gave some of these families a *chassis-level* chance, but **not consistently the same detailed concept-by-concept chance** as donors selected in Steps 04–08.

**The gap is donor-selection discipline, not necessarily the five existing meanings.** It would be unjustified either to discard the draft foundation or to assume the current source choices are optimal. This plan provides a fair, bounded **challenge process**, not an attempt to certify all 182 artifacts or revisit every clause of every standard.

**Corrected selection rule:** *Source type is not an eligibility gate*. For each candidate DIM meaning, ask which recognized standards or donor lineages actually define that meaning, which offer more precise treatment, and which contradict our mapping. **Being too broad to define the overall chassis does not disqualify a donor from contributing an important specialized meaning.** Prior “GREEN/YELLOW,” “core donor,” “companion,” “A/B/C/D,” or “chassis survives” labels are research triage, not binding conclusions that a donor is superior, equivalent, or irrelevant.

## 2. We are challenging five **meaning questions**, not five adopted classes

| Candidate topic from the previous foundation | Question to challenge | Examples of plausible overlooked challengers, **not preselected winners** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F-01 Individual System/subject** | Is the described real-world individual distinguishable from its Node, processor, device type, representation, identifier and hosting/registration roles? | SAPIENT Node; ISA components; SOSA Sensor/host; Redfish management System; IEEE 1451 NCAP/TIM |
| **F-02 Reusable kind/model** | Is our “kind” interpretation technically precise enough for model, node type, transducer self-description, allowed hardware options and role classifications? | SAPIENT NodeDefinition/NodeType; IEEE 1451 TEDS; SOSA device typing; PA-DIM, Redfish |
| **F-03 Method/capability/execution** | Do we preserve declared capability and operating mode separately from chosen configuration, actual execution, detection/task/status and result? | SAPIENT registration/capability/modes/task acknowledgments; ISA interface behaviors; IEEE 1451 transducer services |
| **F-04 Composition/component standing** | Are whole equipment, physical module, addressed transducer channel, configured slot, independently managed child, deployed/hosted Node and digital Component properly distinguished? | IEEE 1451 TIM/TransducerChannel; SAPIENT ConfigurationData/sub_components/dependencies; ISA and SOSA hardware/interface separation |
| **F-05 Digital subject/proxy/identity** | Are hardware/Node identifiers, network processor interfaces, digital middleware, represented subject and source provenance properly distinguished? | SAPIENT middleware and message identity; IEEE 1451 APP/NCAP/TIM; ISA; SOSA SysMan; Redfish/CIM |

A challenger **may reveal a sixth important meaning or a serious gap** rather than fitting an existing row. Record it as **NEW_MEANING_CANDIDATE**, do not invent a sixth accepted DIM class or silently expand this plan. In particular, the [Step 12 audit's suggested operational-condition/status subject](Donor_Alignment/12_Completion_Audit_and_Recommendations.md) remains an **unstarted future option**, not a replacement for the present donor-selection challenge.

## 5. What to report to the owner at the end of **every** iteration

In ordinary language, lead with three explanations: **(1) What did the donor actually say or define? (2) Does that change, sharpen or leave unchanged our candidate DIM, and why? (3) How would that protect or improve the future TRSS/OmniSense/common Resource Details result?** Briefly state the remaining caveats and **what the next single step is**, with a GitHub report and updated tracker link.

Every iteration must read the relevant **exact repository artifact**, check primary donor text where accessible, **commit a named artifact to the research branch**, read back the file and updated status, and stop. The owner will only need to say **“proceed”** to execute the next **single** NOT STARTED step. No repetitive manual approval after the owner already authorized the named step. On a genuine material blocker, record the limited evidence and state whether the next step remains safe. Never claim a tool action happened until commit/readback succeeds.

**Evidence and model hygiene:** Use source-owned technical terms only for donor-native definitions; cite edition, clause/file, originator and status. Disambiguate facts versus V2 inferences, independent donor lineages versus companion artifacts, compatibility in one scope versus **whole-donor conformance**, and **reuse without demanding identical class definitions**. Statuses are distinct: **PRIMARY_TEXT_CHECKED / PRIOR_CITED_RESEARCH / SOURCE_ACCESS_LIMITED / NOT_VERIFIED**; review results are distinct from **NOT_ADOPTED**; proof remains **NO_TRANSFORMATION_TEST** unless actually performed. Preserve the existing SSN 2017/2026 draft IRI caveat and the original corpus of 182. Do not change deployed Glaux, source fixtures, 12-step historical plan, case-study reports, or accept five semantic findings as five classes.
