## 1. What we're trying to deliver, in human terms

The user wants **one generic, profile-aware OGC API – Connected Systems (CSAPI) client and Resource Details presentation** that can interpret unlike **systems, sensors and equipment** from source meaning without manufacturer-name branches. The Profile should support accurate information about *who or what a sensor System is*, what equipment contributes and is connected, what it **can** do, which observations and management actions **actually** happened, what the source says about its history/status/quality, and what it **cannot know**. The ultimate domain name/chassis and the size/cardinality of a formal ontology are **NOT predetermined**. There are **182 recorded donor source artifacts**, **not 182 independently reviewed full standards or normative votes**; later source families can challenge early candidates and never inherit an F-01–F-05 foundation by inertia.

The work intentionally separates (1) the **Domain Information Model (DIM)** of source/technical meanings; (2) the **application Profile** selecting and qualifying actual CSAPI/SensorML/SWE/OMS semantics, original class applicability and missing cases; and (3) the **same generic client interpreter/test** with multiple differently evidenced sources. Phase D reached a qualified documentary conceptual DIM at Step 24. Phase E now has a **coherent candidate Profile** and a full **source-based contract challenge**; the missing source-evidence wire carrier and other conditions prevent a portable interoperability claim. Phase F has **not begun**.
