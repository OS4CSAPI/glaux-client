## 1. What has been reconciled

We are trying to enable a **shared yet meaning-aware CSAPI System Details view** for differently integrated sensing equipment (originating TRSS/OmniSense cases), supported in due course by a *publishable implementation-independent DIM*, an *application Profile*, and a *profile-aware client*. This exercise reconciles already researched meanings; it does not define a new chassis, choose the final domain name, import V1's eight categories as mandatory V2 modules, or replace the actual donor source requirements.

The five candidate distinctions are jointly coherent **only if their relationships and authority remain separate**: (1) physical individual versus identifier/description, (2) product/model/kind versus individual, (3) reusable Method/capability versus actual execution/result, (4) composition/hosting/functional connection versus separately managed component standing, and (5) actual subject versus digital resource, interface, proxy or service. These are **five study topics, not five approved classes**.

## 5. Source-to-UI value and limits

A future profile-aware client should be able to distinguish **what the physical unit is** (actual identity versus product kind), **what parts are really installed** (model options versus observed configuration), **what it supports** (Method capability), **what happened** (actual procedure/Observation/result with correct producing System), and **where these descriptions come from** (resource versus vendor registration, proxy, cached/configured service, with provenance). The current source corpus supports *why these distinctions matter*; it does **not** authenticate an actual Smart Citizen Kit or any end-to-end exchange. TRSS and OmniSense UI fixtures are early synthetic/historical reference cases, not direct live donor conformance tests.

The audited research supports the case for **reuse of existing donor meanings subject to exact qualifications**, not for imposing a single invented cross-donor schema or treating the 182 artifacts as a consensus vote. No new empirical test was carried out in Step 09.
