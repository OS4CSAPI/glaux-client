# AI Hallucination and Research Drift in DIM/Profile V2
## The “operational condition” incident, inherited priorities, and donor fairness

**Version:** 0.1 · **30 October 2026** (America/New_York)  
**Standing:** Owner-requested corrective case study and recovery reference. Not a new research plan, adopted Domain Information Model (DIM), Profile requirement, donor adjudication, or permission to restart closed work.  
**Workspace:** `OS4CSAPI/glaux-client`, branch `research/dim-profile-v2`.  
**Repository baseline examined:** `e83a5909cba747ee298b971af8604b24200c7bc9`.  
**Use in PITONs:** Read this correction before carrying any earlier recommendation into a new chat. Link this document rather than compressing its lesson into “be careful about hallucinations.”

## 1. The finding and its immediate consequence

The assistant repeatedly recommended “operational condition and status,” narrowed to power, as the next direction for V2. When the owner requested its definition and donor pedigree, the trace led to an internal recommendation for future research, not a separately defined concept with provenance equivalent to the five researched foundation meanings. Related donor findings exist, but they establish distinctions among capabilities, reports, modes, actions, results, and diagnostics; they do not establish one synthesized umbrella concept under this name. [R1–R4]

This is a case of **AI hallucination through unsupported conceptual authority, combined with research-direction drift**. The problem is not that electrical quantities or diagnostics are imaginary, or that the phrase can never be useful. A provisional label acquired increasing practical authority through repetition and became the default proposed next subject without a demonstrated V2-wide comparison of alternatives. Repeating disclaimers that no class had been adopted did not prevent that agenda-setting effect.

**Correction:** withdraw the recommendation that power/“operational condition” is already established as V2's best next modeling subject. It remains, at most, one possible question. No donor-defined concept, sixth researched foundation, accepted module, or predetermined next subject may be inferred from that label. Neither a return to TRSS/OmniSense nor a continuation of the V1 power architecture follows from this incident.

The valid V2 research and equipment inventories remain usable. This correction does not erase their findings or declare the five candidate meanings false. It removes unwarranted priority and requires the next choice to be explained from current V2 evidence, competing interpretations, and the equipment data actually available.

## 2. The owner's history and intent — preserve the distinction from verified artifacts

In Profile Rewrite 6 on 30 October 2026, the owner explained that the earlier V1 effort became unrecoverable in his judgment because early-discovered donors shaped the model and later sources could not receive a fair foundational role. V2 restarted from the accumulated resource corpus, not from a mandate to preserve V1's conceptual structure, donor hierarchy, subject order, or preferred examples. This is the owner's account of why he restarted the effort; it is not a claim that this case study independently reconstructed every V1 chat.

He also explained that V2 partly repeated the pattern: important potential primary donors were left out of the initial five-concept work, and the later review was framed as a “challenge” to what already existed. That was not the neutral reconsideration he wanted. His willingness to continue despite risk must not be recorded as agreement that the challenge framing was his preferred method. The six-step challenge and its findings are real historical outputs; their existence does not settle the fairness concern. [R3]

The owner recalls that power received attention in V1 because relevant industry standards, including Internet Engineering Task Force Request for Comments (RFC) documents, were available. The recovered *MERLIN Power Source Domain Model and Main-Profile Binding Recommendation*, v0.1, 14 October 2026, §1.1, specifically recommended RFC 7326 as its organizing framework, RFC 7577 as the battery donor, and RFCs 7460/7461 as companions. That verifies the earlier recommendation's existence, not its current correctness, owner adoption, or priority within V2. The private historical document is identified here without republishing it. [H1]

The owner does not prohibit consulting V1. He requires valuable inherited findings to earn their current role through V2 evidence rather than becoming defaults because they are familiar, well documented, or already implemented. A technically reasonable result can still have been reached by a biased selection process; plausibility is not a substitute for demonstrated provenance and comparative justification.

TRSS and OmniSense motivated the original problem, but the owner now wants better-supported comparable sensing-system datasets for experimentation because adequate complete representations of those two systems are unlikely to be obtainable. Preserve their historical explanatory role without making them the compulsory experimental center or the basis for selecting the next model subject. The desired outcome remains a publishable DIM, a separate OGC API – Connected Systems (CSAPI) application Profile, and meaningful client interpretation—not another cycle of research reports disconnected from those products. [R5; owner clarification]

## 5. The recurring failure mechanisms

**Early-source anchoring.** A candidate model formed from early sources becomes the default. Later sources are asked to fit or defeat it instead of being allowed to generate competing concepts, boundaries, relationships, or organization. Adversarial challenge is useful for testing an intentionally established hypothesis, but is not by itself neutral foundation discovery.

**Promotion through repetition and handoff.** A recommendation becomes a familiar heading, then a presumed information area, then the subject around which a plan is drafted. The document can still say “candidate” while the work sequence treats the candidate as privileged. A PITON can preserve the words accurately yet transmit the wrong level of authority.

**Self-citation mistaken for donor provenance.** A later recommendation cites an earlier assistant-authored report, which itself proposed the subject. That proves the proposal's history, not a donor derivation or that it outranks alternatives. Trace statements back to source-native concepts and clearly identify the assistant's analytical step.

**Task completion substituted for outcome completion.** Eight qualification steps were completed under criteria permitting original published examples. That is not the same achievement as obtaining robust, joined operational datasets. Both statements can be true: the bounded plan is complete, and an important user objective is unmet. [R6]

**Human visibility failure.** Numerous local reports, IDs, caveats, and commits did not provide one readily inspectable account of which concepts came from which donors, how the synthesis changed, and what the datasets can test. Existing tables are useful, but dispersed. Documentation volume did not compensate for an unclear synthesis view. [R3; R8]

## 6. Corrective working rules and recovery instructions

| Risk to prevent | Concrete correction |
| --- | --- |
| An inherited V1 priority returns unexamined | Identify the V1 origin and explain its proposed V2 role using current evidence and alternatives. Reuse is allowed; inheritance is not approval. |
| Later donors must defend against an incumbent | Permit retain, revise, split, merge, replace, or leave open on the same evidence standard. A donor may introduce an unmapped concept or invalidate the current organizing question. No automatic superiority for either early or late donors. |
| The five foundation meanings become an exhaustive schema | Preserve them as current candidate findings, not five immutable slots. Review relevant donor concepts that do not fit them instead of discarding or forcing those concepts. |
| A topical heading looks like a defined concept | Display its definition status and direct donor trail beside the heading. An unresearched question cannot masquerade as a synthesized model element. |
| Donor fairness turns into an endless 182-source exercise | Use a visible relevance/coverage screen at donor-family and source-edition level; explain inclusion, deferral, and review depth. The 182 artifacts are not 182 independent standards or votes. Detailed work can be bounded without hiding omissions. |
| Source relationships become false equivalences | Record the native term, exact edition/clause, direction, scope, qualification, and who asserts the relationship. “Not examined,” “no direct counterpart,” and “incompatible” are different findings. |
| Examples become real observations | Keep design, published example, derived fixture, and acquired operating record separate. An example timestamp is not evidence of an actual event. |
| A data sheet is treated as a full installation dataset | State which component, configuration, message, and time contexts are joined and which are not. Define completeness relative to the experiment; never silently lower the owner's evidence objective. |
| Familiar examples determine the work order | Treat TRSS/OmniSense as historical motivation unless separately selected and evidenced. Use the best currently substantiated comparable cases; record missing dimensions. |
| More process replaces substantive progress | Maintain a readable current synthesis with links to existing evidence, not another duplicate bibliography or control system. Each substantive change should show what meaning improved and why. |

**Carry this paragraph into future PITONs:** V2 restarted from V1 resources, not from V1 architecture or priorities. The 30 October incident showed that “operational condition/status,” narrowed to power, was repeatedly promoted as the next direction without a completed independent concept derivation or comparative V2 priority justification. It is not a sixth researched foundation. The later-donor “challenge” framing did not satisfy the owner's desired neutral reconsideration. A/C/D remain useful reference/example cases, not complete operating datasets; TRSS/OmniSense are historical motivation, not mandatory experimental anchors. Use current V2 donor evidence and explicit data coverage to propose the next action; do not resume the power recommendation automatically.

Closed plans and historical PITONs remain preserved. Read the current README, this correction, the purpose baseline, candidate v0.2, and the data-sheet index together. This correction governs the interpretation of prior recommendations; it does not silently rewrite donor findings, remove evidence, or authorize new model/application work.

## 7. Assessment of the owner's table proposal

**Recommendation, not adopted method:** use a concise, linked concept-and-correspondence table as a central human-readable view of V2 construction, with explanations and source details behind it. The owner pointed to CSAPI Part 1 as a presentation example. The inspected canonical document's §9.2.2 places resource properties beside source vocabulary, definitions, types/targets, and usage; informative Annex C.3 provides another cross-model table. These are useful presentation precedents, not a requirement to copy their semantic or implementation choices into the DIM. [E1]

The pasted URL did not resolve in its exact form. The canonical published HTML was inspected; its original UUID fragment was not independently resolved. The recommendation relies on the specifically identified sections above, not a claim to have verified that exact anchor.

### A central view, with two linked evidence directions

| View | Main question | Minimum visible information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Current candidate DIM and donor correspondences** | What are we proposing, where did it come from, and what remains undecided? | Plain-language meaning; proposed entity/attribute/relationship role; donor-native counterparts and edition links; fit and qualifications; synthesis decision/rationale; unresolved alternatives; candidate/adopted standing. |
| **Donor contributions, including unmapped material** | What does each relevant donor contribute on its own terms? | Native concept and definition; applicable scope; candidate destination or “no destination yet”; relationship/direction; inspected/deferred status; source versus analyst attribution. This prevents the five existing headings from filtering out new contributions. |
| **Equipment evidence and later realization** | Can the proposed meaning represent the actual source information, and what remains untested? | Exact data-sheet row and original record locator; evidence kind; subject/type/unit/time; mapping conditions; preserved/qualified/unrepresented result; later CSAPI/Profile carrier and test status when justified. |

These are linked views of one body of work, not a demand for three new databases or three campaigns. Start with ordinary GitHub Markdown and existing record IDs. Do not make a 182-column spreadsheet or force the reader to interpret codes without nearby explanations. Source-owned cardinalities stay source-owned; proposed DIM constraints need their own rationale, and Profile encoding obligations remain separate.

**Illustration of a decision row, not new model synthesis:** the existing research distinguishes manufacturer model descriptions from OPC UA `DeviceType`, SAPIENT modality/role, and reusable interaction templates. A readable row would show the native names, explain the different scope of each, link to the existing evidence, and leave the candidate structure revisable. It would not merely put checkmarks beneath donors and label them equivalent. Existing reconciliation R09-02 and the later F-02 challenge already provide material for such a row. [R8; R3]

A table supports synthesis but cannot replace definitions, relationships, scope, conditions, counterexamples, and tested transformations. Matching terms does not establish a conversion, and matching a scalar field does not establish a whole-system model. Equally, a well-populated matrix is not proof that every relevant donor received fair consideration; coverage and omissions must remain visible.

## 8. What follows from this correction — not a successor plan

The next defensible recommendation is to **make the existing V2 conceptual result and its gaps inspectable before choosing the next domain subject**. A bounded first application would present the five existing candidate meanings and their recorded alternatives in the proposed table form, include a visible place for relevant donor contributions not yet represented, and connect examples only where their source relationships are already established. This is a recommendation for discussion, not an executed full crosswalk or an assumption that the five should survive unchanged.

That view should distinguish a missing model meaning from missing donor review and from unavailable equipment evidence. These gaps call for different actions. Select subsequent work by its demonstrated contribution to the DIM/Profile objective, cross-source relevance, unresolved foundational impact, and evidence feasibility—not by familiarity with power or a desire to close another numbered plan.

The owner's desired dataset is richer than the acquired reference/example corpus. Existing examples can support bounded conceptual tests, while tests of actual configuration, history, state, or source-to-client fidelity need corresponding real, attributable records. This does not demand impossible knowledge of every device detail; it demands an explicit statement of what is complete enough for each experiment and what remains absent. Do not turn that distinction into either a claim that examples are useless or a reason to quietly abandon the robust-data objective. [R6–R7]
