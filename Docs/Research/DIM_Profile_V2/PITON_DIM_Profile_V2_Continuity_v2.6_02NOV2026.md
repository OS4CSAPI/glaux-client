## 1. The actual objective and deliverables, in human terms

The historical **TRSS and OmniSense** comparison exposed a practical problem: manufacturer-specific sensing System screens can convey identity, attached or integrated sensors, configuration, condition, power, communications, capabilities, measurements and controls, but a **generic** OGC API – Connected Systems (CSAPI) resource listing does not automatically organize these into a useful, faithful experience. The owner seeks **one common, profile-aware Glaux Resource Details experience**, working for unlike sensing Systems, **without vendor-specific special-case interpretation** and without flattening real differences. Source facts may be lost or changed by integration, the selected CSAPI representation, or the client UI; those three potential fault locations must be **tested independently**, not blamed on CSAPI as a whole.

The work has **three deliberately separate products**:

1. **An implementation-independent Domain Information Model (DIM)** of the bounded whole sensing System: real referents and evidence-qualified identity/kind; physical constituents versus external connections; sensing, processing, publishing and management roles; interfaces, channels, properties and report fields; capabilities versus performed activities; status/configuration/control; time, observation quality, provenance and service history. **The right final domain name, chassis superclass, mandatory class set and cardinalities remain to be discovered and tested**, not imposed by V1.
2. **A separate, testable CSAPI application Profile**, selecting legitimate published CSAPI Parts 1 and 2, Sensor Model Language (SensorML) 3.0, SWE Common 3.0, OGC API – Features/other native donor mechanisms as warranted; distinguishing **inherited published-standard conformance** from **new proposed Profile restrictions**, evidence prerequisites and explicit unsupported mappings. The Profile must tell an *unknown producer and client* how to preserve/read meanings, not merely instruct a bespoke Bennett renderer.
3. **One shared, profile-aware Glaux interpreter/Resource Details UI plus an evidence-to-display fidelity evaluation**, exercising differently structured systems through a **single semantic path**. At least two unlike arrangements must be interpreted at the original source level the evidence actually supports; a real historical individual/hardware case separately tests identity and time. Beautiful prototype pages, passing source-fixture checks and actual HTTP reads are useful but each proves **less than cross-vendor interoperability**.

The provisional phrase **“system and sensor and equipment”** remains a **working description**, not a formal adopted domain name. A source-attested focal sensing System/arrangement plus only supported, typed relationships is the qualified Phase C/D working approach; this is **not** a universal “model everything” graph. The original five F-01–F-05 research headings and V1 Managed Physical Sensing Systems (MPSS) history are evidence/candidates, **not the permanent V2 anatomy**.

## 3. What the integrated DIM actually means (Phase D) — preserve the connected model

The qualified [Step 24 DIM](Candidate_Specification/DIM.md#step-24--integrated-source-bounded-whole-system-dim-and-relationship-falsification) begins with **an explicitly named, source-attested focal subject** at **reference design, actual individual, or continuing historic station** standing. A source assertion has issuer, original locator/edition, evidence level, value and time precision. The graph then links independently attested physical participants, functions, interfaces, outputs, performed observing, configuration, controls, maintenance and Host roles **only when justified**. Evidence/derivation applies to each assertion and edge, not only to the focal label. No drawn dotted arrow automatically creates a physical unit or observed activity.

**All 15 integrated R24 propositions remain candidate semantic relations, not OGC wire vocabulary or universal class/property cardinalities:**

| R24 proposition group | Distinction a future consumer must not lose |
| --- | --- |
| **R24-01/02** assertion, kind and representation | Source issuer/identity, real individual vs product kind, a digital proxy vs its real-world subject. |
