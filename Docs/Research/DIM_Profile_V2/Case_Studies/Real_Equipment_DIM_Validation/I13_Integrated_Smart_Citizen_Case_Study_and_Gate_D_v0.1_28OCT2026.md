## Abstract

A Domain Information Model for sensing equipment should describe the System as a complete subject without collapsing its physical identity, product specification, component structure, measurement methods, observations, or digital representations into one another. This case study asks whether five earlier donor-grounded distinctions remain useful when confronted with manufacturer-authored information about the **Smart Citizen Kit 2.3 (SCK2.3)** and whether the resulting small conceptual slice can be accommodated by the published **CSAPI Parts 1–2** and **SensorML 3.0** (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 8–13, 19; 2025b, §§ 7–8; 2025c, §§ 9, 12).

The evidence comprises **four frozen, official source-repository commits**, 25 initially indexed source files, a **60-fact register**, and a targeted independent audit of **34 facts**. The case finds substantive *design-level* value in distinguishing the real-world System from its reusable model, supported Methods from executed Observations, physically integrated equipment from logical sensing outputs, and a manufacturer's software Component record from physical components. A subsequent standards mapping found suitable *conceptual carriers* for the studied distinctions; an adversarial review rejected **four specific naive mapping rules** that would have invented or altered source meaning. **No actual individual physical Kit, verified physical-to-platform identity join, live reading, end-to-end data translation or deployed conformance was established**. The study thus validates the usefulness of these **bounded semantic safeguards**, not the full DIM or empirical interoperability.

## 1. Research purpose, scope and terminology

The motivating requirement inherited from V1 is to understand an equipment-realized sensing System **as a whole operational subject**, rather than only through its measurement streams. "Whole" in this research concerns sufficiently meaningful identity, kind, composition, methods and related information about the *System itself*; it does not mean that every contextual object becomes part of the System or that every conceivable domain is in scope. The methodology remains **purpose-driven but binding-independent**: the DIM explains domain meaning, a future application profile could select and constrain needed information, and a concrete CSAPI/SensorML binding would specify how it is delivered ([Concept Baseline](../../DRAFT_Concept_Baseline_v0.1_25OCT2026.md); [DIM Concept and Method Basis](../../DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md); Pras and Schönwälder 2003).

For the case, the **working CSAPI classification** is an individual **System** whose primary orientation is **Sensor** and whose realization is **Equipment**. These are native, *separate classification facets*, not a newly created combined class or a settled title for the whole V2 domain (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, § 9.2.2). The final domain name, complete scope, classes, module structure, cardinalities, and application-profile obligations **remain open**. The case makes **no claim** that the original 182-donor research corpus has been exhaustively evaluated through this one product or that prior donors such as ISA, SAPIENT, OSUS, OPC UA, AAS and WoT have been discarded.

**Research question:** Can real manufacturer-defined equipment information be kept source-faithful through **source evidence → established donor meaning → candidate DIM interpretation → possible OGC representation**, while avoiding semantic loss, false equivalences and invented evidence? A positive finding about a *mapping possibility* must be distinguished from proof that an identified physical Kit was mapped successfully. This study therefore investigates both the **abilities of the standards** and **the credibility of the inputs** required to use them.

## 2. Case selection, evidence and reproducibility

The Fab Lab Barcelona **Smart Citizen Kit 2.3** is suitable for a bounded *product/design* test because its official documentation describes an environmental monitoring Kit with mating **Data Board** and **Urban Board** assemblies, sensor models, selectable particulate-matter variants, firmware, power/storage mechanisms and a separate data platform. Official schematic/manufacturing artifacts and actual source code permit more than a superficial product-marketing comparison (Smart Citizen 2026, Kit/assembly sources; Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a; [I01 selection](I01_Equipment_Selection_and_Qualification_v0.1_27OCT2026.md)). **It is not suitable for a claim of positive field-unit interoperability on the presently available evidence**, because one identifiable, verifiable physical unit and its readings were not obtained.

The [I02 source register](I02_Versioned_Source_Corpus_Register_v0.1_27OCT2026.md) freezes the following **four official repositories at complete commit identifiers**. The 25 indexed files are an *initial bounded selection* from those commits—not the entire contents of those repositories, a returned production database, or a collection of live device observations.

## 3. The manufacturer-defined equipment and the missing individual

The Kit documentation distinguishes **SCK2.1, SCK2.2 and SCK2.3** product generations, and names several SCK2.3 sensing ICs and supported environmental measurements. Its temperature and relative-humidity channels both refer to **SHT31**, while UV-A, UV-B and UV-C refer to **AS7331**; the available **SEN50/SEN54/SEN55** particulate-sensor models have different supported outputs ([official Kit table at pinned revision](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/kit/index.md#L59-L123); I03 F021–F026). These are genuine **manufacturer statements about kinds, supported options and output characteristics**. They do not identify the specific particulate unit, firmware state or channels active on one physical Kit.

The physical assembly instructions show the Data and Urban Boards joined and secured with a PM sensor connected by cable and **seated in the same enclosure**; the battery and SD card are additional normal assembly/support elements ([pinned assembly instructions](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/guides/getting-started/kit-assembly.md#L114-L149); I07 CMP01–CMP10). The fact that a module uses a cable does **not** make it a temporary external mission payload. Equally, the manufacturer describing separate boards or chips does **not** establish their independently managed CSAPI System identity, actual replacement history or physical persistence for a specific unit.

A notebook in the frozen R source names platform identifiers **18486–18489** for an intended SCK2.3 enclosure test spanning **12 February–7 March 2025**. I04 confirmed **27 notebook cells, 17 code cells, no saved execution counts and no saved outputs**; unsuccessful attempts to retrieve live source data do not prove the platform itself has no records ([pinned notebook](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-data/blob/4f024f4d42cb40c2cd515062ee7e4815bd2798eb/examples/notebooks/learning/01_basic_data_exploration.ipynb#L155-L171); I04 FLAG-05). These numbers are **code references to candidate platform registrations**, **not authenticated hardware identifiers or observed results**. The advanced-station guide's physical Hardware/Station ID versus changeable platform page ID distinction is valuable but **is not a verified identity join for a basic SCK2.3** ([pinned advanced-device guidance](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/guides/getting-started/kit-assembly.md#L256-L273); I03 F047–F051).

## 4. Five donor-grounded findings tested against this equipment

The previous [five-pass synthesis](../../DIM_Profile_V2_Five_Pass_Synthesis_Checkpoint_v0.1_26OCT2026.md) was research about **five distinguishable meanings**, **not five new classes**. I05–I08 applied them to the case. Their contribution is clearest when each is stated with its donor-native role, material source evidence, and empirical ceiling.

| Pass and case-study analysis | Established donor-native role and source finding | What we learned—and did not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Individual System** ([I05](I05_Individual_System_and_Product_Kind_Mapping_v0.1_27OCT2026.md)) | CSAPI **System** represents an individual; its URI-valued **uniqueIdentifier** is distinct from the server resource URL. The vendor has distinct **product**, **registration**, **advanced station Hardware ID** and software record roles (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 8.5, 9.2.2; I05 F001–F004/F047–F050). | **Useful distinction supported.** SCK2.3 is a *kind*, not a serial; no physical individual or positive hardware-to-page ID mapping was verified. |
| **2. Product / model / kind** ([I05](I05_Individual_System_and_Product_Kind_Mapping_v0.1_27OCT2026.md)) | CSAPI **systemKind** points to a Procedure resource **describing a System kind/datasheet**; SensorML **typeOf**, SAREF DeviceKind and AAS Type/Instance offer related but not identical source meanings (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 9.2, 13; 2025b, § 8.2.9.3; European Telecommunications Standards Institute 2024; Industrial Digital Twin Association 2026). | Manufacturer generation, parts and optional SEN5X variants are reproducible *kind-level* information. **No unit-to-kind relation, published CSAPI kind resource or universal ProductModel class is established.** |

A separate [advanced sc_air blueprint](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-data/blob/4f024f4d42cb40c2cd515062ee7e4815bd2798eb/blueprints/sc_air.json#L1-L44) is **not evidence of advanced sensors installed or a result produced by a retail Kit**. **Actual method execution NOT TESTED.** |
| **4. Composition and component standing** ([I07](I07_Composition_and_Component_Standing_Mapping_v0.1_28OCT2026.md)) | CSAPI § 10 distinguishes integral **Composition** from nonpermanent **Aggregation/Deployment** when items merit System-resource standing; SensorML PhysicalComponent/PhysicalSystem process decomposition is a different granularity (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 10–11; 2025b, §§ 8.4–8.6). | Mating boards, mounted chips, an in-enclosure cabled PM module and multiple outputs per chip require **relationship-sensitive descriptions**. No independently identified physical subsystem, unit replacement event, or universal part hierarchy was proved. |
| **5. **The strongest source-based falsifier is the software/hardware Component collision.** A superficially tidy integration could take each backend Component as a nested physical System and create manufactured equipment identities. The actual backend explicitly says it is a device–sensor *database association*, whereas the assembly guide names physical boards, sensor chips and the cabled PM unit. Retaining the five distinct meanings prevents this specific false equivalence **without requiring the source to adopt our own vocabulary** (Fab Lab Barcelona 2026c; Smart Citizen 2026; I07 CMP05, I08 DR05, I10 D01).

## 5. Candidate DIM slice and its modeling boundary

[I09](I09_Candidate_DIM_Conceptual_Slice_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) expressed one candidate **individual System subject** plus role-sensitive relations to kind/model descriptions, implementable Procedures, physical constituents, executed Observations and digital descriptions. Its **C01–C06**, **R01–R06** and **T01–T06** are **analysis/trace IDs, not six DIM classes or a schema**. [I10](I10_Model_View_Critical_Check_and_Gate_C_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) challenged these meanings using source rechecks and nine hypothetical semantic-loss tests, corrected the backend Component wording, and retained **Gate C CONDITIONAL** only for the **textual candidate's internal coherence and source traceability**.

A Unified Modeling Language (UML) class diagram was **deliberately not produced**. A six-box figure would have turned some *roles and unresolved boundaries* into apparent classes, while a physical Kit instance diagram would have needed serial-specific data that were not available; a tiny two-class depiction of CSAPI System and Procedure would add little beyond the standard's actual relation table (I10 § 5; Object Management Group 2017). This is a justified **no-diagram modeling decision**, not evidence that meaningful future UML is forbidden. **The complete V2 DIM, final domain name, modules, logical schema and profile SHALL requirements have not been created or adopted**.

## 6. CSAPI/SensorML accommodation—and what the challenge changed

[I11](I11_CSAPI_OGC_Accommodation_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) assessed twelve possible carrier roles across **CSAPI Part 1 v1.0, Part 2 v1.0 and SensorML v3.0**. It found existing published-resource support for **System instance and identifier**, **systemKind versus implementable procedures**, **ProcessMethod**, **System/subsystem and Deployment**, **DataStream and Observation**, and selected **SystemEvent/configuration** relationships. Some capabilities are in separate requirements classes or richer SensorML encodings: for example, detailed deployed-system configuration in SensorML is **not a base CSAPI Deployment property**, and a SensorML process component is **not automatically an independently identified CSAPI System** (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 9–13, 19; 2025b, §§ 8.2–8.8; 2025c, §§ 9, 12).

[I12](I12_Critical_Interoperability_Challenge_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) then tested twelve interpretive transformations and **rejected four specific shortcuts**. These are failures of *hypothetical mappings*, not executed software or failed conformance tests. They show how a standard may offer a correct carrier even while an incautious transformation into that carrier misstates the original evidence.

| I12 failed naive inference | Correct standards/source reading and significance |
| --- | --- |
| **F01 — Copy the product measurement list into a populated DataStream.** | Part 2 **§ 9.2.2, Table 2** requires **observedProperties, phenomenonTime, resultTime and resultType** to be **derived by the server from linked Observations, or null when none exist**. A manufacturer list of design-supported channels is **not actual DataStream metadata**. Product output descriptions may inform future SensorML process output and stream design, but not counterfeit observed contents (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025c). |
| **F02 — Assume every stream linked from a parent System was directly produced by that parent.** | Part 1 **§ 10.7, Requirement 13/Table 9** includes **subsystems' streams recursively** in parent associations when the Subsystems requirements class applies. The actual producer is identified by Part 2 **§ 9.2.2, Table 5, DataStream.system**. Retrieval under a parent does **not** guarantee direct parent production (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, 2025c). |
| **F03 — Assume every nested subsystem is permanently composed.** | Part 1 **§ 10.5, Requirement 9(C)** allows a subsystem endpoint to contain **currently deployed** subsystems as well as permanently attached ones. Its **§ 10.2** differentiates composition from nonpermanent aggregation. URL nesting or cable attachment alone **does not prove permanence** (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a). |
| **F04 — Treat the backend software Component as a physical subsystem.** | Its native definition is a **Device–Sensor-definition association**, not a physical part. Directly translating it into a CSAPI System would invent independently identified equipment; any actual vendor association-to-output mapping needs more evidence (Fab Lab Barcelona 2026c; I08/I10/I12). |

Additional conditional issues remain: the number and grouping of DataStreams need **actual observations and result schema**, rather than one stream per chip by rule; vendor firmware **GROUP_TIME** is a time of acquisition and must not silently fill *both* phenomenonTime and resultTime; optional stream-level and Observation-level Procedure references do not prove an executed algorithm/version; and registration/page/credential identifiers cannot be assumed to identify the same physical unit. These caveats do **not** prove the standards are deficient; they demonstrate that **semantic preservation depends on source evidence, deliberate mapping decisions and actual tests** (I12 X05–X12; Open Geospatial Consortium 2025c, §§ 9.2, 9.6–9.7).

The resulting assessment is **no confirmed novel semantic defect in CSAPI/SensorML for the small slice examined**, **not** proof of universal standards completeness or lossless translation. A real service might implement only some defined requirements classes, and no deployed Smart Citizen CSAPI endpoint, live Observation or conformance response was tested. The study therefore **does not justify an invented DIM class, generic proxy-equivalence predicate, mandatory component hierarchy, or new normative profile statement**.

## 7. Evidence-backed contribution to the V2 program

The case has **three defensible contributions**. First, it shows that the five researched distinctions are not merely theoretical labels: the Kit's genuine product options, physical assembly, firmware stages and vendor backend model offer concrete examples where a careless integration would make **false assertions**. Second, it exposes the difference between **standards-level semantic accommodation** and **source-faithful implementation**, using normative rules on DataStream metadata, recursive production associations and subsystem discovery to falsify four tempting shortcuts. Third, it establishes an inspectable path from immutable source claims through donor-native meanings into a **small, binding-independent candidate DIM**, without importing vendor software structure or CSAPI resource layout as intrinsic domain classes.

That is meaningful research progress, but it **does not complete the original whole-system model**. Identity/model, component standing, methods, digital representation boundaries and the perimeter of actual observations were studied; capability/performance, configuration, state, power, communications, deployment, condition, service/lifecycle and the wider donor-accommodation envelope were **not comprehensively modeled or validated here**. The V1 eight information subjects remain *requirements evidence*, **not** predetermined V2 modules; neither the earlier quarantined anatomy Phase B/C/D results nor a new compound root/class has been resurrected ([DIM Concept and Method Basis](../../DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md); [prior PITON v1.4](../../PITON_DIM_Profile_V2_Continuity_v1.4_26OCT2026.md)).

**What the research adds to a future profile** is not a premature SHALL. It is a better-supported understanding of **which meaning must be preserved** when existing standards are selected, where descriptions need different scopes, and which producer, identity, procedure and component relationships would require explicit traceable evidence. The profile can later draw interoperability obligations from a substantiated use case; it must not determine the underlying DIM's semantics merely because a convenient JSON field, API endpoint or vendor table exists.

## 9. Conclusions and recommended next research decision

The Smart Citizen Kit investigation demonstrates **why a whole-System DIM needs carefully distinguished meanings** even when the examined OGC standards already contain many of the necessary semantic constructs. In this particular source corpus, a credible product design, a documented physical assembly, measured-property definitions, source routines and a web platform all exist—but they do **not** become one identified individual merely by putting their names into a common hierarchy. The study's principal outcome is therefore **preventing wrong assertions with source-grounded distinctions**, not demonstrating an already completed interoperable model.

The result does **not** support declaring V2 finished or starting normative profile writing. It supports retaining the **five bounded candidate meanings and their traceability**, carrying the I12 constraints forward into any later implementation, and seeking a positive physical-to-platform identity and observation evidence chain **if empirical interoperability with this Kit is to be claimed**. Separately, a future source-grounded study of **capability and performance** is the next substantive conceptual family suggested by the prior [five-pass synthesis](../../DIM_Profile_V2_Five_Pass_Synthesis_Checkpoint_v0.1_26OCT2026.md); that suggestion is **not** evidence that new modules or classes are already warranted.

**Recommended immediate next action (after this completed 13-iteration plan):** A short **post-case-study research decision checkpoint** to assess **(a)** whether a targeted real-unit evidence acquisition/mapping test is feasible and worth pursuing, and **(b)** the next bounded donor-grounded DIM concept investigation (beginning with capability/performance), while preserving their different purposes and evidence limits. That decision checkpoint must **not** be treated as an automatic I14, a model-adoption vote or authorization to begin both tasks. The project owner may authorize it with **“proceed.”** The [PITON C](PITON_C_Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) preserves the exact restart procedure.
