## 1. Candidate slice, purpose and boundary

**Candidate conceptual statement.** For the present equipment-realized sensing case, the central subject of description is an **individual System**. Information about its reusable kind/model, the Procedures or Methods it can implement, meaningful constituent elements and their association to the whole, and the digital resources through which it is described **has different referents and relationship meanings**. An actual Execution or Observation is again a different occurrence. The available Smart Citizen evidence supports **manufacturer product/design descriptions, firmware method descriptions, documented assembly relationships, and software-representation structures**, but **does not establish any particular physical Kit as an individual, its installed configuration, its link to a platform record, or an actual Observation** (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 8.5, 9.2.2, 10.1–10.3, 13.1; Open Geospatial Consortium 2025b, §§ 7–8; Open Geospatial Consortium and International Organization for Standardization 2023, § 8; Smart Citizen 2026; Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a, 2026b, 2026c).

The **semantic distinctions** in this statement follow source-native donor concepts; the way they are **selected and placed next to each other for this use case** is a **V2-INFERENCE**, not an agreed cross-donor ontology. The slice is deliberately a **small, typed conceptual description and relationship set**, not a package of new classes or five modules.

**Universe of discourse for this exercise:** an individually distinguishable sensing-oriented System physically realized as equipment, considered as the whole operational subject rather than only its observations. The present OGC API – Connected Systems (CSAPI) characterization **systemType = Sensor** plus **assetType = Equipment** is a *working target-scope test*, not a new composite class, universal root, or final domain name. Hardware can include computation, storage, power and communication without all its parts being Sensors (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, § 9.2.2; Open Geospatial Consortium 2025b, § 8.6; [concept baseline](../../DRAFT_Concept_Baseline_v0.1_25OCT2026.md)). The broader final V2 boundary remains discoverable and open.

**What this slice deliberately does not try to model:** capability/performance as a complete family; live configuration or state; maintenance and replacement events; Deployment, hosting or installation history; whole observation/result schemas; command/management interactions; power/communications submodels; digital twins/proxy architecture; universal identity reconciliation; or normative CSAPI profile constraints. These subjects may matter to the eventual *whole-system* DIM but cannot be inferred merely from five earlier research passes or a single product design.

The ID labels **C01–C06 identify this note's candidate inventory entries**; they are **not URI terms, classes, entities, normative requirements, or source-native identifiers**.

| Entry | Meaning selected for the candidate slice | Donor-native pedigree | Evidence standing and model treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C01 — Individual System (anchor)** | A particular equipment-realized sensing System is the subject, distinct from any identifier, product family, reading, API resource, or record about it. A whole may perform non-sensing support functions. | CSAPI individual **System** and role/realization facets (§ 9.2.2); SensorML physical-process/system scope (§ 8.6); SOSA/SSN individual System distinction (21 Oct 2026 **Working Draft**, §§ 5.3.5, 8.16). | **Candidate central meaning, not a new IndividualSystem class.** Manufacturer evidence identifies the **SCK2.3 design**, but **no particular physical SCK2.3 System** is established. No instance, serial/UID, location or actual configuration is populated. |
| **C02 — Reusable kind/model *description role*** | Information describing a type, generation or specification potentially shared by more than one individual. A source may offer a model name, datasheet, type asset or kind archetype at **different levels of detail**. | CSAPI **systemKind** targets a kind-describing **Procedure** resource (§§ 9.2.2, 13.1); SensorML **typeOf**; SAREF **DeviceKind**; AAS Type/Instance Assets (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, 2025b; European Telecommunications Standards Institute 2024; Industrial Digital Twin Association 2026). | **Supported for product-design descriptions** such as SCK2.3 and SEN5X options; **unpopulated as an association from a verified individual**. A *role*, **not** an adopted generic ProductModel class or assumption that these donor objects are equivalent. |
| **C03 — Reusable Procedure/Method** | A description of how a task or transformation can be performed, separable from both the performer and a historical performance. No identified Procedure resource, installed-method claim, or actual execution. Use existing donor term **Procedure**, not a new SensorMethod class. |
| **C04 — Constituent description and relationship standing** | Parts or subprocesses may be meaningful to describe, but their **relation to the whole** and their **independent descriptive/System standing** are separate questions. Not all parts or measurement channels become Systems. | CSAPI full-System **subsystems**, integral **Composition** vs non-permanent **Aggregation** (§ 10); SensorML **PhysicalComponent/PhysicalSystem** and process connections (§§ 8.4–8.6); SOSA/SSN **hasSubSystem** vs hosting/Deployment (2026 Working Draft). | **Supported at design level** for Data/Urban boards, mounted ICs and in-enclosure cabled PM module; **not verified** as an inventory of a fielded Kit. Entry denotes a **modeling distinction**, not a universal Component superclass, part-of predicate, nested hierarchy or UML diamond. |
| **C05 — Execution/Observation (perimeter)** | An actual performance, its used Procedure, producer and result are different from reusable methods or product capabilities. | OMS Observation/Observer/ObservingProcedure (2023, § 8); SOSA/SSN Execution/usedProcedure/madeBySystem (2026 Working Draft); CSAPI Part 2 Observations (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025c). | **Conceptual boundary only, not populated or independently elaborated here.** No real SCK Observation/result or method-use provenance was retrieved. Example notebook source and its date range **cannot fill this role**. |
| **C06 — Digital description/resource (boundary)** | A registration, API database record, field, document, channel association, service or proxy may **describe or expose information about** equipment without being the equipment. These carriers may describe different scopes and use different identifiers. | CSAPI real-world UID vs resource URL (§ 8.5); AAS Shell vs represented Asset; WoT Thing/Thing Description/Intermediary; OPC UA Device vs AddressSpace node; SAREF Service vs Device Function (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a; Industrial Digital Twin Association 2026; World Wide Web Consortium 2023; OPC Foundation n.d.; European Telecommunications Standards Institute 2024). | **Explicit non-conflation boundary**, **not** a DIM DigitalRepresentation class or universal representedBy relation. Smart Citizen's backend **Device** and **Component** are source-specific software constructs; no digital record was verified as referring to a particular physical Kit. |

**Crucial separation:** C02 and C03 are *semantic roles*, not a declaration that their underlying source documents are mutually disjoint classes. **CSAPI itself uses one Procedure resource type in two distinct association roles**: **systemKind** describes the kind of System; **procedures** describes methods the System can implement. Likewise C04's *physical constituents* are not the same construct as the vendor backend C06 **Component** record, even if the English word matches (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 9.2.2, 13; Fab Lab Barcelona 2026c, backend code cited in § 4).

## 3. Candidate relationships and source-granularity decisions

These are **semantic relation statements for review**; the row IDs **R01–R06** are analytical identifiers only. There are **no adopted V2 property names or multiplicities**. The status describes *what is supported in this particular case*, not a conformance grade.

| Relationship/role | Narrow meaning carried into the candidate model | Case mapping and evidence status | Boundary/alternative left open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **R01 — Individual → reusable kind description** | A System *may be characterized by* reusable information about what kind/model it is; a kind description is not the individual. | **Product side SUPPORTED**: manufacturer SCK2.3 generation and technical specification (I03 F001–F004, F020–F026; I05). **Individual-to-kind join NOT VERIFIED**. | CSAPI **systemKind**, SensorML **typeOf**, SAREF **hasDeviceKind**, AAS type/instance patterns are **candidate correspondences**, **not** one mandated class/field/cardinality. |
| **R02 — Individual → Procedure that it can implement** | Described capability to follow a reusable method is separate from the type description and from an actual event. | **Methods SUPPORTABLE FROM SOURCE**: SHT31 command/read/CRC/convert routine, conditional SEN5X routine, AS7331 acquisition/scale (I06 M01–M04). **A specific individual implementing them NOT VERIFIED**. | Whether/how to identify, version, publish or link each method is open. A firmware function is not automatically an independent Procedure resource. |
| **R03 — Execution → performer / method used / result** | A historical occurrence must be distinguished from the System's supported procedures, from a buffered reading's format, and from platform upload/receipt. | **NOT TESTED**: I03 F058–F059 notebook has no saved outputs; I06 M05/M08 show source fields but no observations. No actual occurrence or method-use link is asserted. | Included as a **boundary and unpopulated relation**, not a complete Observation/Execution schema or a DIM-wide cardinality. |
| **R04 — Whole → constituent / non-constituent context** | A known **physical assembly** may have parts, but an enduring built-in part, maintenance-replaceable assembly, temporarily attached payload, physical host and network/data-flow relationship are **not interchangeable association types**. | **Product-design relation SUPPORTED**: Data Board + Urban Board joined; PM module cabled **inside normal enclosure**; mounted SHT31/AS7331 and other ICs; battery/SD (I07 CMP01–CMP10). **Actual unit parts, swaps and permanence NOT VERIFIED**. | CSAPI Composition/Aggregation applies **only when the relevant thing merits System standing and the relation meaning is evidenced**. Cabled does not imply temporary Deployment. SensorML process component does not imply independent hardware System. |
| **R05 — Physical constituent vs measurement channel vs software Component** | A chip/module and a measurable property/channel can be described differently; the backend's record associating a platform Device with a sensor definition is **not evidence of one separately identified physical circuit**. | **POSITIVE DISAMBIGUATION**: same SHT31 carries temperature + humidity; same AS7331 carries UV-A/B/C (F021/F024; I07 CMP05). Pinned backend **Component belongs_to device and sensor** (I08 DR05; direct A-source). | Do **not** instantiate physical subsystems from digital **Device.components** entries, assert one channel per chip, or infer a universal Component class from a vendor field name. |
| **R06 — Subject ↔ evidence-qualified digital description** | A service record or digital description is not identical to its equipment referent; **whether it concerns a particular individual requires an evidence-backed link**. | **Role distinction SUPPORTED**: station Hardware ID versus changing platform device ID in advanced-device guidance; backend **Device**, **Component**, token and CSV/API views (I08 DR02–DR08). **Physical-to-digital association NOT VERIFIED** for any basic SCK2.3. | Treat correspondence as **unresolved binding/identity/provenance work**, not an invented DIM **representedBy** property, universal proxy class or presumed one-to-one relation. |

The **minimum informational commitments** earned by this exercise are **role-sensitive differentiation** and **source-qualified association**. That means a model or its later binding must be able to *avoid asserting* that a product name is a physical UID, an available method was executed, a channel is a separately deployed component, a cabled sensor is necessarily temporary, or an API representation *is* the hardware. **This is a candidate information-integrity discipline, not a new set of DIM or profile SHALL statements.**

## 5. Source granularity, choices rejected and alternatives deferred

**What can be described now, without pretending to have a specific Kit:**

- **Manufacturer product-design view:** the **SCK2.3 generation** and its documented board/sensor options; common parts and characteristics apply to a design, not a fabricated serial-specific unit.
- **Documented process view:** inspectable source-level acquisition and data-processing routines, with qualified model/firmware applicability; not actual device use.
- **Assembly view:** board assemblies, mounted chips, and an in-enclosure cabled PM module as documented *physical design*, without a mandatory recursion of managed subsystems.
- **Software representation view:** platform/backend **Device** and **Component** roles, their sources and potential readings, without claiming they describe an authenticated physical Kit.

**The following joins have no positive evidence and therefore remain deliberately unpopulated:** particular physical Kit ↔ kind/model and installed revision; Kit ↔ individual PM module/parts; Kit ↔ active firmware/configuration and method; Kit ↔ physical/advanced Hardware ID; physical Kit ↔ platform **Device** record; Kit/method ↔ historical execution/Observation/results; and Kit ↔ verified CSAPI, AAS, OPC UA, WoT or other deployed standard-specific representation. This does **not** assert that these data do not exist in Smart Citizen's operational environment.

**Alternatives considered, and why no one is adopted in I09:**

| Candidate design choice | Disposition and reason |
| --- | --- |
| New **IndividualSystem**, **ProductModel**, **SystemComponent** or **DigitalRepresentation** classes | **NOT ADOPTED.** Existing donor concepts explain the selected roles; the case supplies no demonstrated gap requiring those V2-owned classes. |
| One generic kind-and-method superclass or two universally disjoint classes | **NOT ADOPTED.** CSAPI reuses Procedure resources for two different roles; OMS acknowledges that a Procedure may describe a sensor type. Model **role meanings** first, not forced packaging. |
| Full component tree with universal **partOf**, recursive System identity, or UML composition diamonds | **NOT ADOPTED.** Source-native physical board/chip/module detail is not equivalent to independently identified System subsystems; CSAPI Composition is expressly looser than UML composition. |
| Treat all output channels or backend **Component** objects as physical hardware parts | **REJECTED FOR THIS SOURCE.** D01 and A backend show non-equivalence directly (T04). |
| Promote **hardware ID = platform ID = MAC = UUID = token = CSAPI UID** | **REJECTED AS UNSUPPORTED.** Identifiers have different known roles, and the positive equivalence evidence is absent. |
| Generic **representedBy** relation, digital-twin/proxy class, one-record-per-System rule | **DEFERRED / NOT ADOPTED.** Donors represent different scopes and cardinalities; actual digital identity join missing. Binding/provenance might be the right layer. |
| A UML class diagram now | **DEFERRED TO I10.** A two-box diagram would add no information beyond R01–R06 and could silently convert roles/boundaries into classes. I10 will specifically evaluate a faithful, value-adding model/view or record a no-diagram decision. |
| Broader capability, configuration, power, state, deployment and maintenance modules | **OUTSIDE THIS SLICE.** Valuable whole-System topics, but neither I09 nor the five-pass study has researched their complete semantics. Do not import V1's eight subjects or quarantined earlier Phase B/C/D anatomy as model architecture. |

**No final multiplicity, datatype, identifier syntax, universal time/identity persistence rule or normative obligation follows from this note.** Published CSAPI requires a System **uniqueIdentifier**, but its independence/persistence wording is **SHOULD**, not a universal lifetime **SHALL**; that is a *binding-source rule*, not proof that we have identified a physical Kit (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 8.5, 9.2.2). AAS **assetType** (link to a type Asset) is not CSAPI **assetType = Equipment**. The **2026 SOSA/SSN text is a Working Draft**, not the published edition normatively referenced by CSAPI 1.0.

## 6. I09 result and the I10 critical test

**Result earned:** There is now a **bounded conceptual information-model slice**, with one candidate System anchor, differentiated kind/method/constituent relationships, explicit execution and digital-representation boundaries, six inspectable R## semantic statements, six trace rows from frozen manufacturer/software evidence through donor-native meanings into V2 interpretation, and **no fabricated physical individual or observation**. **What has been modeled** is the *meaning that the information would need to preserve*, not populated hardware instances or standard-specific API resources.

**What is genuinely unresolved:** Whether separate source-identifiable constituents deserve individual System standing, whether a source-neutral digital identity relation belongs in the DIM or only a binding/provenance layer, how model/method versions and time-scoped component relations should work, what actual values/observations are linked to the selected physical subject, and whether these roles can be shown accurately as a *formal UML class view* without inventing semantic commitments.

**Evidence gates:** **Gate A remains CONDITIONAL** (documented design only); **Gate B remains CONDITIONAL** (five-pass source-consistency and error prevention only). **Gate C is NOT YET EVALUATED**: I10 must independently test this candidate model against source meaning and drawing/notation hazards; it may revise or narrow I09 rather than rubber-stamp it. No fielded Kit, empirical interoperability, or profile conformance has been verified.

**Next: Iteration I10 — Model/View and Critical Check.** Review R01–R06 and T01–T06 against the original donor and case evidence, decide whether a UML class/instance view expresses additional *supported* meaning or whether the present matrix/text is more faithful, record discrepancies and corrective disposition, update the tracker, and stop before I11. One user **“proceed”** authorizes I10 only.
