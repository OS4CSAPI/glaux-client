## 1. The answer from real equipment evidence

**There is more than one meaning of “how the Smart Citizen Kit works.”** The manufacturer's SCK2.3 product description identifies **what kinds of environmental measurement its design supports**. Its pinned firmware gives much more specific **ways particular sensors are read and their values processed**. The Kit's configuration controls whether and how frequently supported sensors are used. Its flash and network paths **record, buffer, and distribute** acquired values. A distinct Smart Citizen **air-quality processing blueprint** gives instructions for computing derived gas-concentration values from other raw readings, but is **not evidence that the basic SCK2.3 has those electrochemical sensors** (Smart Citizen 2026, F025–F029, F039–F044 and F051–F057; Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a, H05; 2026b, R02).

These distinctions test the central Pass 3 finding:

> An individual sensing **System** may be capable of implementing a reusable **Procedure/Method**, while a particular **Execution/Observation** is a separate occurrence that *actually used* a procedure. A manufacturer product model, a sensor-support switch, a configured sampling interval, a timestamped result, and a data-upload protocol do **not all mean Procedure** merely because they participate in obtaining data (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 9.2.2 and 13.1; Open Geospatial Consortium 2025b, §§ 7.1–7.2 and 8.3.2; Open Geospatial Consortium and International Organization for Standardization 2023, §§ 8.2 and 8.5–8.6; World Wide Web Consortium and Open Geospatial Consortium 2026, §§ 5.3.3–5.3.5).

**What is established:** The frozen source contains **real, inspectable algorithmic and procedural detail**. **What is missing:** no authenticated fielded Kit, loaded firmware/configuration, executed measurement, returned platform record or result-to-method relationship. Gate A remains **CONDITIONAL**. Nothing in this iteration demonstrates that the upstream code actually ran on one of the prospective platform IDs 18486–18489.

## 2. Direct evidence — exactly which native methods and stages are present

### 2.1 Measurement routine in firmware — temperature and humidity

The manufacturer's SCK2.3 measurement table identifies an **SHT-31** temperature/relative-humidity sensor, F021 ([D01, SCK2.3 table](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/kit/index.md#L86-L91); Smart Citizen 2026). In the pinned hardware/firmware repository, **`SckUrban::getReading`** selects sensor-specific acquisition routines ([H05, acquisition dispatch](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-kit-2x/blob/3dbbc1061509920aca2dda2aca32abe82563500b/sam/src/SckUrban.cpp#L198-L229); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a). The **`Sck_SHT31::update`** implementation sends a named **`SINGLE_SHOT_HIGH_REP`** measurement command, reads a six-byte response, checks cyclic-redundancy checks for the raw temperature and humidity words, and converts the raw values into temperature and relative humidity ([H05, SHT31 update](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-kit-2x/blob/3dbbc1061509920aca2dda2aca32abe82563500b/sam/src/SckUrban.cpp#L842-L895); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a). The separate **`Sck_SHT31::getReading(SckBase*)`** path also applies temperature/humidity offsets, including additional adjustments conditioned on USB charging ([H05, reading/correction paths](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-kit-2x/blob/3dbbc1061509920aca2dda2aca32abe82563500b/sam/src/SckUrban.cpp#L929-L969); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a).

**Donor-neutral interpretation, not a new class:** This is **actual source-code evidence for a specified acquisition/conditioning method**; far more informative than the product label “SCK2.3 measures temperature.” The manufacturer's embedded routine does **not by itself establish** that a separately published, independently identified **OGC Procedure** exists, reveal every physical operating principle inside the SHT31 chip, prove the offsets used by a particular Kit, or demonstrate a historical Observation. **Nor is a C++ function name a DIM class or a universal procedure identifier.**

### 2.2 Alternative particulate-matter measurement routine — sensor-dependent stages

The official SCK2.3 product documentation explicitly differentiates **SEN50, SEN54 and SEN55** options: the base PM functionality and optional VOC/NOx-index functions are not identical (F025–F026; [D01, SEN5X model variants](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/kit/index.md#L120-L123); Smart Citizen 2026). The pinned source's `SCK23_AIR` configuration includes the SEN5X code path ([Sensors.h, SCK23_AIR switches](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-kit-2x/blob/3dbbc1061509920aca2dda2aca32abe82563500b/lib/Sensors/Sensors.h#L35-L41); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a); the general `SckUrban::getReading` dispatch calls a sensor-specific SEN5X handler ([H05, SEN5X dispatch](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-kit-2x/blob/3dbbc1061509920aca2dda2aca32abe82563500b/sam/src/SckUrban.cpp#L258-L273); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a). More significantly, **`Sck_SEN5X::getReading`** uses internal states for starting a measurement, handling a warm-up period, checking for a fresh reading, selecting update/continuous behavior, and returning a value only after the relevant conditions are handled ([H05, SEN5X acquisition path](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-kit-2x/blob/3dbbc1061509920aca2dda2aca32abe82563500b/sam/src/SckUrban.cpp#L2022-L2100); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a).

The official power/interval guidance says a **roughly 15–20-second PM stabilization** affects the recommendation not to configure a basic sensor interval below **30 seconds** unless accepting different continuous-power behavior ([D04, recommended interval rationale](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/kit/features.md#L22-L27); Smart Citizen 2026).

**Mapping limit:** This supplies **implementation and operating-method evidence**, including warm-up and conditional behavior. But a compile-time option, the manufacturer's recommended interval, and a real unit's current sensing procedure are **three different claims**. We do **not** know which SEN5X variant, firmware build, calibration or intervals any physical Kit used. We do not infer that every SCK2.3 supports NOx merely because some compatible PM variants do.

### 2.3 Ultraviolet channel acquisition and conversion

The SCK2.3 product table names **AS7331** for UV-A, UV-B and UV-C (F024; [D01, UV channel identification](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/kit/index.md#L92-L96); Smart Citizen 2026). The frozen firmware's **`Sck_AS7331::getReading`** begins measurement, checks device-ready/error flags, obtains raw register values and calculates separately scaled UV-A/B/C outputs using stored gain/integration settings ([H05, AS7331 reading and scaling](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-kit-2x/blob/3dbbc1061509920aca2dda2aca32abe82563500b/sam/src/SckUrban.cpp#L2760-L2815); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026a).

**What this contributes:** We have evidence that a source-defined **method includes an algorithmic transformation**, not merely a sensor name. The actual integration-time/gain values used by a specific unit, its measured UV value, and whether a particular source-repository version was running remain **unknown**. A future model may need to preserve which method and parameters apply without demanding a new standalone object for every firmware operation.

### 2.4 Configuration and sample handling are related but different

The manufacturer's firmware guide says supported sensors can be **auto-detected and enabled conditionally**, and the **Shell** can change enabled sensors, sampling frequency and operating mode (F040–F041; [D07, detection and Shell configuration](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/firmware/index.md#L16-L45); Smart Citizen 2026). Those settings can **affect how a method is carried out**. They are not themselves proof that a fielded unit used a particular method, and do not automatically identify a new method for each setting.

The same guide specifies that acquisition groups in flash have **`GROUP_TIME`** identifying the time of reading and **`GROUP_NET` / `GROUP_SD`** flags identifying publication status (F042, F044; [D07, reading timestamp and publication flags](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/firmware/index.md#L88-L107); Smart Citizen 2026). It distinguishes storage in internal flash from later SD or MQTT handling, subject to time synchronization and configuration ([D07, sampling, buffering and publishing conditions](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/hardware/firmware/index.md#L43-L53); Smart Citizen 2026). The separate platform documentation says SCK data may arrive through MQTT while HTTP serves application/import paths (F052–F054; [D10, ingestion pathways](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/data/data-platform.md#L30-L35); Smart Citizen 2026).

**Meaning:** *When the equipment acquired a reading* cannot be replaced without evidence by *when a server received, forwarded, or exported it*. **The difference matters to observation provenance.** But a firmware binary-buffer field is a source-specific representation mechanism, **not automatically an OGC Observation execution or a Procedure class**. No concrete recorded timestamps were retrieved for our case.

### 2.5 Advanced air-quality processing is a distinct source-owned method

Smart Citizen's calibration guide distinguishes sensors whose data are processed **on the sensor** from **advanced electrochemical air-quality sensors processed on the platform** using hardware-ID-dependent information (F029, F050–F051; [D09, processing-location distinction](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/guides/data/handling-calibration-data.md#L3-L20); [D09, advanced processing and hardware versions](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-docs/blob/0a18bc0bd372d2bcedfca5bc5e6109f88ea8519f/docs/guides/data/handling-calibration-data.md#L38-L72); Smart Citizen 2026). The separately frozen **`blueprints/sc_air.json`** gives explicitly named **processing methods**, including `ASPT1000` depending on `PT1000_POS` and `EC_SENSOR_TEMP` depending on `ASPT1000` (F056–F057; [R02, process and temperature dependencies](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-data/blob/4f024f4d42cb40c2cd515062ee7e4815bd2798eb/blueprints/sc_air.json#L1-L44); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026b). It also defines a derived **NO2** channel with a function named `alphasense_803_04` and declared dependencies on raw electrode values and electrochemical-sensor temperature ([R02, NO2 processing inputs and function](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-data/blob/4f024f4d42cb40c2cd515062ee7e4815bd2798eb/blueprints/sc_air.json#L193-L206); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026b).

This is **especially clear source evidence of a reusable, parameterized processing *description***, and of a meaningful distinction between raw acquired signals and computed output values. The blueprint's reference to `AAN 803-04` is a **name/documentation reference in source code**, **not an independently audited external standard or a validated mathematical algorithm**.

**Critical boundary:** `sc_air` is a **separate advanced-air-quality processing blueprint**, not evidence that a standard retail SCK2.3 carries electrochemical sensors. The official notebook **uses that blueprint** for test code named `SCK2.3_ENCLOSURE_TEST` (F058), but its **17 code cells have no saved execution output** (F059; [R01, reference-only notebook source](https://github.com/fablabbcn/smartcitizen-data/blob/4f024f4d42cb40c2cd515062ee7e4815bd2798eb/examples/notebooks/learning/01_basic_data_exploration.ipynb#L159-L170); Fab Lab Barcelona 2026b). **It does not establish that any candidate platform record had such hardware, ran this algorithm, or produced any reported result.** This is a *related source-method demonstration* with conditional applicability, **not proof of an installed procedure on the base Kit**.

## 4. Donor semantics—what fits and what only partially fits

**OGC API – Connected Systems Part 1, v1.0:** A System's **`procedures` association** may refer to Procedures it can **implement**. Its separate **`systemKind`** relation describes the *kind of System*, even though **both use the standard's Procedure resource type as a carrier**. Thus the manufacturer's SCK2.3 datasheet (I05) and the source-defined SHT31/SEN5X/AS7331 acquisition methods (I06) serve **different descriptive roles**. But **no published manufacturer CSAPI Procedure resource or deployed SCK2.3 CSAPI System association has been demonstrated**. We have only a defensible semantic correspondence (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025a, §§ 9.2.2 and 13.1–13.2).

**SOSA/SSN, 21 October 2026 Working Draft:** `sosa:implements` relates a System to a reusable Procedure it is capable of executing; `sosa:madeBySystem` and `sosa:usedProcedure` apply to a **particular Execution**. This makes the evidence tiers above meaningful: a stored sensor routine or postprocessing blueprint might offer sufficient **source description for a method candidate**, but the absence of a verified unit and an actual observation prevents an empirical **“System X executed Procedure Y at time T”** assertion (World Wide Web Consortium and Open Geospatial Consortium 2026, §§ 5.3.3–5.3.5). This mapping retains the [**exact 21 October Working Draft**](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/WD-vocab-ssn-2023-20261021/) reviewed in Pass 3, rather than silently substituting a later working-draft edition; it is **not the edition published CSAPI 1.0 normatively cites**.

**OGC/ISO Observations, Measurements and Samples (OMS) 3.0:** Its distinction between an **Observer**, the **ObservingProcedure** it can follow, and an **Observation** made by using a procedure is directly relevant to the question of acquisition method and result interpretation. The source's observation-to-procedure requirements belong to **OMS's model**, not an invented universal V2 cardinality. **No OMS observation was created here** (Open Geospatial Consortium and International Organization for Standardization 2023, §§ 8.2, 8.5–8.6).

**OGC SensorML 3.0:** A `ProcessMethod` describes how a `SimpleProcess` transforms inputs/parameters into outputs; more complex process structures and connected subprocesses may supply methodology without a single standalone ProcessMethod. SHT31 command/read/check/convert and the conditional SEN5X routine exemplify source-reported detail that could motivate a process-method representation, while the off-device `sc_air` channel-dependency structure could motivate a **separate processing method/process** description if actually used. We have **not generated SensorML**, adopted specific SensorML classes as DIM classes, or proved any specific Kit implements those hypothetical encodings (Open Geospatial Consortium 2025b, §§ 7–8.4).

**Why not mint a new `SensorMethod` or `SystemProcedure` class?** The donor corpus already provides **Procedure/Method** semantics; the current case raises questions of *how to link, identify and specialize source methods*, not demonstrated evidence that all those donor concepts are insufficient. We are preserving their separate meanings and differing source granularities.

## 5. Concrete interoperability test—and the limit of the proof

Consider **one hypothetical SCK2.3 operating with SHT31 and SEN50** (not an identified fielded unit). Source evidence gives: a reusable Kit model/technical description (I05); firmware routines for temperature/humidity conversion and SEN5X warm-up/PM reading (M01/M02); options to enable sensors and set timing (M04); an acquisition timestamp distinct from storage/publication indicators (M05); and a published data-platform transport path (M06).

A careless integration could **lose or invent meaning** by saying: “SCK2.3 implies the same method on all units,” “30 seconds was the actual sampling interval,” “the source code's firmware version was definitely installed,” “the upload timestamp is the acquisition time,” or “the unexecuted notebook proves an air-quality algorithm generated a historical reading.” **None of those claims follows from the evidence.**

Our mapped source distinctions explain **why those specific errors must be avoided**, and suggest where method/parameter/provenance information would be useful to an eventual DIM and profile. But this is **a conceptual evidence test**, not empirical demonstration of a live reading or an end-to-end CSAPI interchange. The individual-to-method and observation-to-used-method relationships remain **not tested**.

## 6. Disposition, open questions, and next action

**I06 result:** **SUPPORTED** — method-level content exists in real, version-pinned firmware and an explicit processing blueprint. **PARTIAL** — donor semantics can accommodate reusable method descriptions and possible capability links, but no standard-native Procedure resource, authoritative method UID/version binding, or individual equipment/installed configuration has been established. **NOT TESTED** — actual physical unit, executed observation, input parameters, calibration and result-to-method provenance. These limits are a continuation of **I04 Gate A**.

**Open questions to carry forward, not to answer by invention:** When a firmware routine vs a manufacturer methodology should be referenceable as an individual Procedure; how method/version and chosen parameters relate to each fielded System; whether advanced postprocessing is performed by software service/another System rather than the primary sensing equipment; and which observation/result metadata are needed to establish that a particular method was *actually used*. None is settled by the fact that code or transport specifications exist.
