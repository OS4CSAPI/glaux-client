## 1. Executive verdict — what actually survived an attempt to break I11?

**I11's high-level conclusion survives only with important qualification:** Existing CSAPI/SensorML constructs provide suitable meaning for *the studied distinctions*, but **that does not demonstrate a source-faithful data transformation**. Some easily imagined source-to-OGC transformations would be **affirmatively incorrect under the standards**, even though the standards themselves expose the information needed to avoid the mistakes.

**Four naive mapping rules fail on direct source/standard evidence:**

1. **Product capability → populated DataStream metadata.** Part 2 § 9.2.2 requires observedProperties, phenomenonTime, resultTime and resultType to be **server-derived from linked Observations**, and to be **null if there are none**. The SCK2.3 manufacturer's measurement list cannot be substituted for observed stream facts.
2. **Parent's DataStream association → parent is the measurement producer.** Part 1 § 10.7 requires parent System associations to include descendant subsystems' DataStreams recursively when subsystems are represented. Part 2 § 9.2.2 identifies the DataStream's own **system** association as the producer. A parent-level link is therefore not by itself a provenance assertion.
3. **Present under nested subsystems → permanently composed.** Part 1 § 10.5, Requirement 9(C), explicitly allows the subsystem endpoint to also expose **currently deployed** subsystems. Hence nested discovery does not by itself establish lifecycle permanence; distinguish the § 10.2 Composition/Aggregation criteria and the applicable Deployment evidence.
4. **Vendor backend Component → physical CSAPI subsystem.** The pinned Smart Citizen backend defines Component as a **Device–Sensor-definition database association**, not a physical circuit or a one-channel-per-chip relation. Converting it directly into a System would invent physical System standing.

These are **failures of the specific attempted inference rules**, not failed physical integrations and **not identified missing CSAPI standard features**. Additional choices remain conditional (method provenance, result grouping and schema, sensing-vs-processing time, constituent granularity and source identity). Empirical claims remain **UNTESTABLE** because the corpus does not authenticate any actual individual SCK2.3, its API record or an actual result.

**Revised defensible I11 verdict:** **No confirmed new CSAPI/SensorML *semantic deficiency* in this bounded, product/design-level slice; NO PROVEN LOSSLESS TRANSFORMATION OR EMPIRICAL INTEROPERABILITY.** The existence of a conceptual carrier is weaker than a fully specified, correctly implemented and tested mapping. An unfilled source field is neither a mapping success nor proof of a standard's deficiency.

## 2. Critical test protocol and decision labels

For each candidate mapping, challenge: **(1)** What did the manufacturer/source mean at exactly which level—product design, software code, database association, physical individual, actual execution? **(2)** What does the approved OGC source actually require or permit? **(3)** Could the transformation *preserve the original meaning*, *change the referent*, *omit necessary information*, or *invent values*? **(4)** What concrete evidence would prove or falsify the result?

**Statuses in this audit** are not counts of deployed test executions:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **SUPPORTED** | The relevant source-native distinction and OGC role are independently evidenced; no instance or implementation is automatically certified. |
| **CONDITIONAL** | A legitimate OGC carrier exists, but the source-specific mapping, grouping, optionality, version, timing or required data is not sufficiently established to claim lossless transfer. |
| **FAILED—NAIVE RULE** | The **hypothetical mapping rule or inference**, not the standard, is contradicted by an exact original source or normative OGC requirement. |
| **UNTESTABLE** | Cannot verify actual cross-system records/results because a necessary physical individual, source record or timestamped observation is absent. |
| **UNKNOWN** | Current evidence does not establish whether a source requirement demands semantics beyond available OGC carriers; retain as an open question, not an asserted defect. |

**Test method:** Clause-by-clause critical reading of CSAPI Parts 1–2 and SensorML 3.0, targeted rereading of the **commit-pinned** Smart Citizen physical assembly, measurement table, firmware buffering, backend Component and advanced-station identity documents, and checking I04's original source-audit limits. **No actual source payload was transformed, no server queried successfully for the candidate devices, no hardware operated and no conformance tests run.** The counterfactual transformations below are explicitly **tests of interpretation**, not fabricated source events.

## 4. Substantive corrections and qualifications to I11

**Correction C12-01 — Part 2 DataStream metadata is observation-derived, not product-derived.** I11 A05 and § 5 correctly meant that manufacturer outputs may *inform the design* of later stream representations. They must not be read as permission to **populate** DataStream **observedProperties, phenomenonTime, resultTime or resultType** from a product table without linked real Observations. Part 2 § 9.2.2 expressly says these are server-generated from observed members, or null where none are linked. This is a meaningful **carrier restriction omitted from I11**, and affects any future positive example.

**Correction C12-02 — parent System associations can expose descendant data.** If a true System hierarchy is modeled, **P1 § 10.7 Table 9** requires the parent System's **datastreams** association to include streams produced by its subsystems recursively. I11's discussion of independent component/System standing was correct but did not mention the resulting **source attribution trap**. Resolve **P2 DataStream.system** instead of treating the retrieval path or parent association as the direct producer. This should be an explicit I13 interoperability warning if retained.

**Correction C12-03 — nesting does not prove permanence.** The P1 § 10.5 subsystem endpoint **must** expose permanently attached subsystems but **may** expose currently deployed ones. The source-defined permanence distinction lives in **§ 10.2** and evidence of component lifecycle/Deployment. I11 was correct that CSAPI Composition and Aggregation are different, but a reader must **not infer Composition from nested discoverability alone**.

**Qualification Q12-04 — output grouping and time need observed evidence.** A DataStream refers to a **single System output**, with observations sharing source/observed properties/result schema (P2 §§ 9.1–9.2); an Observation **may** contain multiple properties, and per-stream/per-format schemas are provided via § 9.6. One physical chip does not dictate DataStream count or provenance. Likewise **GROUP_TIME** is not automatically both observation time attributes. These are **unresolved transformation design questions** under I11 A04/A05, rather than proven failures of OGC representation.

**Qualification Q12-05 — optional method metadata may be too weak for a stricter future need.** CSAPI Part 1 supports optional implemented-method links; Part 2 supports optional stream/observation used-Procedure links and optional observation parameters. Their presence in the model is **not proof** that actual heterogeneous source results carry sufficient algorithm/version/calibration provenance. A future V2 profile might need to select stronger expectations if a concrete integration requirement and real source evidence demand it—but **nothing in this case yet establishes a universal new SHALL**.

**No corrections to I04's prior source-fidelity adjudication:** the old claimed sensor part-number discrepancy stays **RETRACTED**, and the separate S25FL064L 8 MB design corroboration does not establish installed storage on an individual unit. **I03's original F001–F060 identifiers are unchanged.** I11 should carry a visible review notice pointing here, not be rewritten into an apparent originally perfect result.
