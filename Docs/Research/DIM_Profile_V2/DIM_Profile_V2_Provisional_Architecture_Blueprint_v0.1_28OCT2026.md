# DIM / CSAPI Profile V2 — Provisional Architecture Blueprint

**Version:** 0.1 · **28 October 2026**  
**Standing:** **Explanatory, source-led architecture map for owner review.** It identifies the *current subject, researched semantic foundations, candidate information areas, cross-cutting needs, and potential specialization*, **without adopting a UML class structure, modules/packages, a final DIM scope/name, or a CSAPI Profile**. It is **not** a second validation of the old anatomy Phase B/C/D; those outputs remain **quarantined**.  
**Repository/branch:** \`OS4CSAPI/glaux-client\` / \`research/dim-profile-v2\`. The live UI is independently maintained on \`work/details-view-20261023\`.  
**Authority:** [Original problem and intended outcome](DIM_Profile_V2_Problem_and_Outcome_Baseline_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) and [PITON v1.5](PITON_DIM_Profile_V2_Continuity_v1.5_28OCT2026.md). **This document is one conceptual map, not a mandate for a new research campaign or an approved sequence of implementation tasks.**

> **In plain language:** We are trying to give a CSAPI client one intelligible **System Resource Details** experience for different real sensing-equipment systems, beginning with the originally studied TRSS and OmniSense, while keeping the source-specific meaning that the vendors' own interfaces communicate. The proposed route is **recognized donor semantics → coherent DIM → CSAPI application Profile → faithful integration → profile-aware client UI**. The five research passes are an **initial foundation**, not a completed model or the entire core.

**Reading rule:** The diagram's words are *analytical labels*, not donated class names, UML associations, CSAPI resource declarations, DIM packages or future Profile requirements. Some concepts are well supported as **distinctions**, while their ultimate **model position**, relation name, cardinality, specialized vocabulary and code-level binding remain unresolved. We should permit real modular structure to emerge, **not draw arbitrary module boxes and then make the evidence fit them**. This follows the [DIM method, §§ 4–9](DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md), [modeling convention](DIM_Profile_V2_Modeling_Convention_v0.1_26OCT2026.md), and [visual guide, § 2.3 and § 2.5](DIM_Profile_V2_Modeling_and_Visualization_Guide_v0.1_25OCT2026.md).

## 2. Working chassis and the initial common foundation

**The investigated subject is a real-world System**, not the CSAPI URL that returns its description, the manufacturer product definition, or a measurement channel. The current **working, not final** boundary concerns equipment-realized Systems whose primary role is observing/sensing, including non-sensing internal functions. For CSAPI, \`systemType = Sensor\` and \`assetType = Equipment\` are **two independent classification facets**; neither implies a new compound DIM class. The domain boundary is a separate question from its future model root. Refer to [concept baseline](DRAFT_Concept_Baseline_v0.1_25OCT2026.md), [cross-group chassis result](DIM_Profile_V2_Chassis_Cross_Group_Validation_and_Decision_v0.1_25OCT2026.md), and [182-source reconciliation](DIM_Profile_V2_Chassis_Corpus_Wide_Reconciliation_v0.1_25OCT2026.md).

**These are the five foundations already researched, not five adopted classes or five exhaustive core packages.**

| Researched candidate meaning | What the DIM may need to say, in source-neutral language | Key donor pedigree already examined | What is *not* yet decided | Why the Details UI benefits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Individual System identity** | The particular subject differs from its URI, identifier, kind, events and digital records; its description may evolve. | [Pass 1](DIM_Profile_V2_Canonical_Concept_Pass1_Individual_System_Identity_v0.1_26OCT2026.md): CSAPI System, SOSA/SSN System, SAREF Device, AAS asset, OPC UA Devices. | New \`IndividualSystem\` class, universal lifetime/replacement rule, identity reconciliation. | Display a real System's name/ID/source without confusing the resource record or a model name with the equipment. |
| **2. Product/model/kind** | A reusable description may characterize multiple individuals; it is not the individual or the observing algorithm. | [Pass 2](DIM_Profile_V2_Canonical_Concept_Pass2_Product_Model_Kind_v0.1_26OCT2026.md): CSAPI \`systemKind\`, SensorML \`typeOf\`, SAREF DeviceKind, AAS Type/Instance, WoT Thing Model and OPC UA type distinctions. | One universally interchangeable \`ProductModel\` class, inherited-property precedence or compulsory model-to-instance association. | Show documented vendor model capabilities separately from actual installed configuration and reported condition. |
| **3. Procedure/Method** | A reusable way of doing something differs from the System able to perform it, from configuration, and from an actual Execution and result. | [Pass 3](DIM_Profile_V2_Canonical_Concept_Pass3_Procedure_Method_v0.1_26OCT2026.md): SOSA/SSN Procedure, OMS, SensorML ProcessMethod, distinct CSAPI \`procedures\` vs \`systemKind\`. | One class per firmware function, method-resource obligation, executed-method proof without an actual observation. | Present supported methods or acquisition behavior without implying a command occurred or measurement was made. |
| **4. Composition and component standing** | The System may be atomic or composite; separate *relationship meaning* (part, host, attached, deployed, connected) from *description granularity* (chip, channel, module, subsystem). | [Pass 4](DIM_Profile_V2_Canonical_Concept_Pass4_Composition_and_Component_Standing_v0.1_26OCT2026.md): CSAPI Composition/Aggregation, SensorML, SOSA/SSN, OPC UA Devices and equipment case evidence. | Universal Component superclass, mandatory component tree, subsystem identifiers for every chip, UML diamonds based on CSAPI terms. | Keep TRSS detector slots, OmniSense transducers and Smart Citizen board/chip/channel arrangements meaningful rather than falsely identical. |
| **5. Digital representation/proxy identity** | A server record, Thing Description, software association, proxy or twin may represent/mediate the equipment without *being* the physical subject. | [Pass 5](DIM_Profile_V2_Canonical_Concept_Pass5_Digital_Representation_and_Proxy_Identity_v0.1_26OCT2026.md): CSAPI System/resource, AAS, WoT, OPC UA, SAREF and OSUS-R. | A universal proxy/twin class, one-to-one digital-to-physical join or identical IDs across vendors/servers. | Preserve source/proxy provenance and avoid showing database rows as distinct fielded sensors. |

**Maturity statement:** The [five-pass synthesis](DIM_Profile_V2_Five_Pass_Synthesis_Checkpoint_v0.1_26OCT2026.md) connects those meanings into one *candidate semantic understanding*. The later [I09 candidate relationships R01–R06](Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I09_Candidate_DIM_Conceptual_Slice_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) and [I10 critical check](Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I10_Model_View_Critical_Check_and_Gate_C_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) show narrow coherence and necessary qualifications. **R01–R06 are research IDs, not UML associations or adopted properties.** They do not settle the eventual scope of the reusable core.

## 3. The whole-system coverage map: candidates for modules, core rules or specializations

Our **Version 1 eight information subjects** are legitimate *requirements evidence*, **not** eight V2 modules. The [DIM method, § 6](DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md) explicitly says to retest their cohesion and allow different architectural outcomes. This matrix serves as our **coverage inventory and map of possible organization**, not a declaration that all rows will become separately named packages.

| Candidate information area inherited as a **question** | Present evidence and coverage | Architectural alternatives to examine; **none selected** | What the next evidence must discriminate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Identity, kind, technical description** | Strongest researched candidate distinctions: individual vs kind vs proxy; existing technical-description prototype precedent. | Shared common semantics, with technical-description specialization if justified. | Which details really apply to all in-scope Systems; how provenance, source IDs and inherited kind information are scoped. |
| **Equipment, composition, physical arrangement** | Pass 4, Smart Citizen I07 and I09: boards, cabled enclosure PM module, chips and output channels are *not* interchangeable kinds of parts. | Common relationship meanings plus an optional deeper equipment/assembly area, if justified. | When a part merits separate identity, how actual installed equipment differs from documented product design, service/replacement relationships. |
| **Sensing capabilities, performance, outputs** | A model/Method distinction is researched; I06/I07 document sensor outputs and algorithms. **No complete V2 capability/performance information model yet.** | Potential reusable sensing/instrumentation specialization or other cohesive model area. | Function vs capability vs method vs performance specification vs selected mode vs measured result; units, conditions and uncertainty. |
| **Deployment, emplacement, location, responsibility** | CSAPI/SOSA provide context relationships; earlier chassis work rejects treating host/deployment as intrinsic composition. No consolidated V2 context model. | Shared typed context relations; an independently coherent deployment/operational-context module only if evidence warrants. | Time-dependent relationships, platform hosting, direct/indirect membership, geospatial location, operational assignment and responsibility. |
| **Operating configuration, modes, actions** | Source examples and I06 distinguish supported methods from actual execution. **A full configuration/command lifecycle is not modeled.** | Common configuration/state distinctions, optionally specialized controls and action semantics. | Supported vs selected vs requested vs applied setting; version, effectivity, controls, permissions, command/status/result roles. |
| **Communications, connectivity, access** | Donor corpus and predecessor MPSS/client work contain relevant descriptions. **No independently accepted V2 module or complete common model.** | Typed interfaces/links in shared meaning plus optional rich network/communications model. | Equipment interface vs network/service/proxy path; identity/address, current connectivity vs design capability, security/access boundary. |
| **Power sources, operating arrangement** | Substantial **predecessor** power-source modeling and Glaux power fixture; Smart Citizen identifies product battery/charging design. Not an adopted V2 module. | Potential cohesive Power/Energy module with System-level relationship endpoints and source-qualified properties. | Power provider/consumer/interface roles, electrical metrics vs estimated capacity, actual configuration and replaceability over time. |
| **Condition, health, service, recovery, lifecycle** | Original UGS examples contain reported condition and estimator values; I09–I12 stress non-conflation. **No general V2 condition/maintenance architecture accepted.** | Possibly typed, cross-cutting state/evidence pattern with separately coherent service/maintenance specializations. | Reported state vs derived assessment vs maintenance event; source time/quality, fault/health definitions, lifecycle consistency. |

**Not lost between these eight rows:** actual **Observations, DataStreams, Commands, CommandStatus/Results, SystemEvents, other Executions and derived evidence** are real distinctions in the CSAPI/OMS/SOSA environment. They may touch several information areas; treating them as “a ninth module” would be premature. [I12 X01–X07](Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I12_Critical_Interoperability_Challenge_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) already demonstrates why a product capability is not actual DataStream observation metadata and why a parent-associated stream need not have been *produced* by that parent.

**What the table intentionally does NOT claim:** that the listed areas collectively exhaust the future domain, that a row necessarily becomes a module, or that any donor standard implements the entire area. The eventual model should be more specific than a flat list of topical screens and less rigid than an eight-package structure imposed before research. New evidence may combine, split, discard or add areas.

## 4. Three organizational mechanisms worth preserving—but not pre-assigning

**Shared core (possible future common model content).** Reuse a concept once **only** when the semantic invariant really applies across relevant Systems. Candidate examples include the distinction between individual subject and product kind; a method and an actual execution; constituent relationships versus deployment context; and physical subject versus digital representation. This does **not** prove one root class, one common status field, or identical component trees. [DIM method, §§ 5, 7–8](DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md).

**Coherent reusable modules (potential).** A Power, Communications, or other module should be added only when it has (i) a clear independently explainable cluster of concepts and relationships, (ii) bounded dependencies, (iii) exact supporting donor semantics, (iv) concrete application beyond one source quirk, and (v) tested preservation of relevant source meaning. **No module is approved by this blueprint.** A UI tab is not a DIM module; a standards family's terminology is not automatically a V2 module name. The [modeling/visualization guide, § 2.3](DIM_Profile_V2_Modeling_and_Visualization_Guide_v0.1_25OCT2026.md) explicitly prohibits a UML package diagram before actual package boundaries have been earned.

**Specializations and cross-cutting qualification (potential).** UGS detector/cue/tamper/sleep details, radiation instrumentation, scientific measurement devices or industrial process sensors may legitimately need additional concepts. Preserve specialization where commonality would distort their native meanings. Across whatever model elements emerge, *identity scope, time/validity, quantity/units, method/version, quality, provenance and responsibility* need consistent treatment **where supported and applicable**, not invented always-present fields or a predeclared “qualification module.” This is a **design need** supported in several donor and case studies, not a completed universal qualification metamodel.

**Architecture admission test:** Before calling anything a *core concept*, *reusable module*, *specialization* or *cross-cutting pattern*, state its domain meaning **independently of CSAPI encoding and UI layout**, cite exact donor-native concepts and any non-equivalent mappings, show source-level counterexamples, identify its relationship/dependency and scope, and explain **what interoperability or presentation failure it prevents**. When these requirements cannot be met, retain the area as a **candidate question**, not a class, package or normative assertion.

## 5. One worked vertical thread: why this architecture could matter

This is the **traceability bridge** missing from much of our earlier research. An example does not itself define DIM classes or Profile rules:

| Layer | TRSS ETU-II example | OmniSense example | Smart Citizen 2.3 case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Original meaning** | Two configured detector positions, selected types and battery-estimator inputs. | Core2 source-reported transducer labels; **separate** Core5ADS historical reported voltage. | Product-design Data/Urban Boards, in-enclosure cabled PM module, SHT31 temperature+humidity and AS7331 UV-A/B/C. |
| **Donor-grounded conceptual distinction** | Configured detector *position* vs chosen equipment type vs possible hardware instance; estimated capacity ≠ electrical measurement. | Reported label ≠ authenticated independently identified part; reported voltage ≠ battery-capacity percentage. | Physical chips and modules vs outputs; product kind ≠ actual Kit instance; backend \`Component\` database association ≠ physical part. |
| **Candidate DIM contribution** | Preserve meaningful configuration/slot association and status of estimate, not a fake identified unit. | Preserve equipment report and measurement qualification without merging Core2 and Core5ADS. | Preserve model/whole/part/channel and software representation boundaries, with explicit product-kind evidence. |
| **Potential Profile/encoding question — OPEN** | Which existing CSAPI/SensorML representations, definitions, value roles and groupings would let a client recognize a detector slot and estimator input? | Which consistent terms and qualifiers communicate reported equipment/condition without claiming unsupported identity or time? | How should a model kind, components, multi-output sensors and optional PM variants be published without asserting real individual/observation records? |
| **Common Details view test** | **Operational / Equipment** can show the two detector positions; **Condition** can label a percentage as *estimate*. | Same frame can show five transducer labels; other source case shows **15.43 V DC** with clock caveat. | Same frame can group boards, SHT31 and AS7331 with distinct measurement channels, plus design-vs-instance warning. |
| **Limit** | Synthetic historical fixture, not actual configured live device. | Two separate synthetic source cases; no verified combined state. | Pinned manufacturer product/design information, **no authenticated physical individual or observations**. |

The right-direction deployed experiment is [Glaux Profile-aware Resource Details](https://glaux-client.pages.dev/?page=resource-details-profile-test), documented in the [app's design record](https://github.com/OS4CSAPI/glaux-client/blob/work/details-view-20261023/Docs/Design/RESOURCE_DETAILS_PROFILE_TEST.md). Its local TypeScript rulebook is **only a candidate renderer convention** and **not** an adopted DIM or a discoverable CSAPI Profile. The owner's assessment was that it is **janky but heading in the right direction**. The earlier [Smart Citizen research-commentary page](https://glaux-client.pages.dev/?page=resource-details-smart-citizen) was not the intended test and remains separate.

**Success later** means faithful original-vendor/source → documented conceptual interpretation → valid profile-constrained CSAPI/SensorML System response → client render, with **preserved / qualified / unavailable / lost / misinterpreted** decisions observable. It does not mean that a client must reproduce every proprietary vendor UI pixel-for-pixel. [Purpose baseline, §§ 1, 5–6](DIM_Profile_V2_Problem_and_Outcome_Baseline_v0.1_28OCT2026.md).

## 6. Where this blueprint belongs in the eventual two publications

The project already has separate [provisional document outlines](DIM_Profile_V2_Provisional_Document_Outlines_v0.1_26OCT2026.md). We should not confuse **this map** with either final artifact.

| Future destination | Material it can receive when substantiated | What this blueprint does **not** populate yet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DIM: purpose/scope + concept overview** | Source-backed domain boundary and candidate common meanings, later expressed as suitable definitions and associations; eventually readable UML views. | Final domain name, root class, adopted UML diagram or complete class inventory. |
| **DIM: definitions, relationships, shared/specialized organization** | Carefully reviewed language from the five passes; further verified concept families as their semantics and boundaries become clear. | Accepted definitions/association names/cardinalities, packages or requirements merely by being listed here. |
| **DIM: pedigree and governance** | Existing donor-native definitions, exact source references, versioned mappings, alternative interpretations, source limits and candidate dispositions. | A claim that compatibility with a donor is the same as implementing or conforming to that donor. |
| **CSAPI Profile (separate)** | Once justified: selected DIM meanings, inherited CSAPI obligations, semantic term usage, representation/encoding conventions and any necessary assessable new requirements. | Normative SHALLs, conformance classes, Profile identification/version rules, complete mappings, Abstract Test Suite. |
| **Client/integration proof** | Tests that the Profile actually lets source-specific System details be rendered coherently and faithfully. | Real interoperable CSAPI endpoint, actual devices, automated end-to-end profile conformance, finished UI. |

**Publication discipline:** The DIM is **implementation- and binding-independent**, while the application Profile selects/constrains meanings for CSAPI; the CSAPI/SensorML encoding realizes those rules and a compatible client interprets the result. A domain model should not be reverse-engineered from current UI tabs, JSON array layouts or a convenient endpoint. Conversely, model semantics need an eventual interoperable binding if they are to improve real client behavior. See [concept/method basis, §§ 3 and 9](DRAFT_DIM_Concept_and_Method_Basis_v0.1_25OCT2026.md) and [publication outlines](DIM_Profile_V2_Provisional_Document_Outlines_v0.1_26OCT2026.md).

## 7. Current state, unanswered questions and review criteria

### What is actually established

- **Working domain/chassis boundary**, source-led but provisional; no invented overarching class or final name. The 182-source corpus is research infrastructure, **not 182 independently verified universal equivalences**. See [chassis reconciliation](DIM_Profile_V2_Chassis_Corpus_Wide_Reconciliation_v0.1_25OCT2026.md).
- **Five donor-grounded candidate semantic findings** with detailed evidence and cross-source pedigree; **no adopted class model**. A later [five-pass checkpoint](DIM_Profile_V2_Five_Pass_Synthesis_Checkpoint_v0.1_26OCT2026.md) synthesized them, but did not turn them into five formal classes.
- **One completed Smart Citizen 13-step source-grounded test**, with Gate A/B/C **CONDITIONAL** and Gate D **CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE OF RESEARCH ONLY**. [I13](Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I13_Integrated_Smart_Citizen_Case_Study_and_Gate_D_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) and [I12 adversarial challenge](Case_Studies/Real_Equipment_DIM_Validation/I12_Critical_Interoperability_Challenge_v0.1_28OCT2026.md) show both value and four false mapping shortcuts. Empirical device/CSAPI interoperability was **not tested**.
- **Early meaning-aware UI illustration**, not live CSAPI Profile-aware operation. [App design record](https://github.com/OS4CSAPI/glaux-client/blob/work/details-view-20261023/Docs/Design/RESOURCE_DETAILS_PROFILE_TEST.md). Owner feedback: *on path, far from finished*.
- **A documented, standards-informed modeling convention** favoring textual definitions and focused UML class diagrams **once modeling decisions merit them**. This map is expressly **a context diagram and coverage matrix**, because drawing packages/classes now would overstate the evidence.

### Major open modeling questions

1. **Common core completeness:** Beyond the five already studied, which other distinctions truly belong in the shared model and which are specialized? Especially capability, performance, configuration, state and actual activity.
2. **Real modularity:** Which whole-system information areas form coherent modules with independently meaningful relationships and bounded dependencies, and which instead belong in common relationships or qualifications?
3. **System/part granularity:** Under what evidence is an internal chip, subsystem, transducer, replaceable module or externally connected System separately identified and how is its lifetime relation represented?
4. **State and time:** How do design capability, configured mode, effective state, reported condition, derived assessment, actual Event/Observation and data quality remain clearly distinguishable?
5. **Profile commitment:** Which CSAPI/SensorML structures already suffice, where are shared recognized vocabularies/bindings needed, and which rules genuinely require profile-authored testable obligations?
6. **UI fidelity:** Can the same profile-aware client component render original TRSS/OmniSense semantic meaning from **actual valid CSAPI resource representations** without special-case product code or invented facts? Which vendor details remain unsupported or missing?
7. **Scope and donor compatibility:** Where are different donor concepts genuinely equivalent, merely mappable with qualification, or incompatible? How do we preserve the relationship without promising full donor implementation or conformance?

### How to judge this blueprint before any architectural promotion

A future review should be able to answer **what is the System**, **what are the researched common meanings**, **where are the larger unanswered information needs**, **why could modularity be useful**, **where does vendor specialization fit**, and **how does any of it reach a CSAPI Profile and an intelligible client**—**without** interpreting any table row as a class, module, profile requirement, or conformance assertion. If any answer is unclear, revise this map's explanation rather than imposing architecture prematurely.

**No next research pass is automatically authorized by this document.** The limited, owner-approved task was **to reconstruct one useful provisional architectural blueprint from existing work**. Future work can use the above open questions to select a concrete problem, document the source-to-DIM-to-Profile-to-UI implications, and only then decide whether additional targeted donor research is necessary. The old [quarantined anatomy Phase B/C/D](PITON_DIM_Profile_V2_Continuity_v1.5_28OCT2026.md#6-frozen-constraints-never-regress) remains prohibited as factual model authority.
