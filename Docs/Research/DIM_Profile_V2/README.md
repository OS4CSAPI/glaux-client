## Current bounded evidence-corpus plan — STEPS 01–05 COMPLETE

The [**Controlled Sensing-Equipment Corpus Qualification Plan v0.1**](PLAN_DIM_Profile_V2_Controlled_Sensing_Equipment_Corpus_Qualification_v0.1_29OCT2026.md) defines **eight one-prompt iterations** to select a small, reproducible, quality-controlled public equipment corpus before further whole-System DIM research. It screens candidate unattended sensing systems, deep-audits **one system per prompt** (four audit slots), selects **2–3 source-qualified primary cases if the evidence warrants**, and freezes exact provenance and sample-data availability. A claimed product feature, public sample payload and actual field-recorded observation retain **distinct evidence standing**. **Steps 01–05 are complete; Steps 06–08 are NOT STARTED. Eight candidates were screened; A and C have bounded reference/example PASS verdicts; B is CONDITIONAL and not qualified as a primary case; D remains unaudited. No final corpus has been selected.**

The committed [**Step 01 eligibility gates and reusable assessment template**](Controlled_Corpus/01_Eligibility_and_Evidence_Gates.md) define four minimum gates, the plan's six evidence labels, shared comparison questions, six evidence-depth dimensions and PASS/CONDITIONAL/FAIL verdicts. A documented reference configuration and original published examples can support bounded research without being presented as an authenticated installation or actual telemetry. A conditional case is not a qualified primary case, and scores cannot compensate for a failed gate. These are **research-method criteria, not adopted DIM classes or Profile requirements**.

The [**Step 02 discovery report and four audit slots**](Controlled_Corpus/02_Discovery_and_Four_Audit_Slots.md) assign **A: AirGradient Open Air Max; B: Raspberry Shake & Boom; C: Dragino S31-LB external-probe/battery node; D: Campbell CR1000X + first-generation ClimaVue 50 reference arrangement**. Dragino replaces the initial ZL6 audit to broaden structural/interface coverage. **METER ZL6 + ATMOS 41 and AudioMoth remain credible reserves, not failed candidates.** Surveyor and McQ RANGER were screened as closer UGS comparators; usable field-defined public messages were not located in the bounded review. Public specifications and original examples were inspected, but no real station dataset or complete evidence packet was acquired. Exact model/firmware/record applicability remains for the audits.

The [**Step 03 AirGradient audit**](Controlled_Corpus/03_Candidate_A_Evidence_Audit.md) gives **PASS only for the documented O-M-1PPSTON-CE reference assembly and original published examples**. Hardware and firmware descriptions are independently pinned, not claimed as a verified installed release pair; the commercial `-S` suffix is not silently equated. The [small attributed offline checker](Controlled_Corpus/03_AirGradient_Example_Check.py) verifies the illustrative cellular structure and a comment-stripped Wi-Fi derivative, not real telemetry. **Actual observations/history score: 0.** Lab endpoint retrieval failed in this environment; configuration state, comprehensive diagnostic records, live integration and calibrated gas validity are not established. Timing/field ambiguities and inconsistent PDF renderings are explicitly bounded. This is evidence eligibility, **not final corpus selection or an adopted DIM/Profile**.

The [**Step 04 Raspberry Shake & Boom audit**](Controlled_Corpus/04_Candidate_B_Evidence_Audit.md) gives **CONDITIONAL: G3 UNVERIFIED; not a qualified primary case**. It identifies a source-described combined V3 instrument and the separate manufacturer-attributed historical station AM.R9D92.00, but waveform/response downloads were not successfully inspected. Browser format limits and runtime DNS failures are not evidence that the files are absent. Other-model UDP/test examples were not substituted. **Actual observations/history score: 0.** Local status/configuration, response applicability and exact installed state remain unverified; published reuse restrictions prevent assuming permission to rehost waveform data in CSAPI/Glaux. The bounded audit is complete, not an automatic retry campaign.

The [**Step 05 Dragino S31-LB audit**](Controlled_Corpus/05_Candidate_C_Evidence_Audit.md) gives **PASS only for the external-probe/primary-battery reference product and original published examples**. The complete eight-entry historical-message example was inspected and processed with a pinned manufacturer decoder; the [offline checker](Controlled_Corpus/05_Dragino_Example_Check.cjs) separately labels all synthetic tests. It records timezone-dependent calendar strings, no-data markers becoming apparent zero measurements, misleading convenience labels and manual arithmetic discrepancies without changing the upstream code. Decoder filename/version and synthetic status values do not establish a deployed firmware or region. **Actual observations/history score: 0.** No installed state, successful command, live telemetry or final corpus is established; S31B/LS construction is not borrowed.
