## Maintained Smart Citizen experiment paper

**Evaluating Sensor Information Preservation Through OGC Integration**, version
**0.6**, dated **24 October 2026**, is the working paper for the proposed experiment.
Read the [canonical manuscript](Studies/Smart_Citizen/MANUSCRIPT.md) or
[generated Word edition](Studies/Smart_Citizen/SMART_CITIZEN_EXPERIMENT_PAPER.docx).
The [paper guide](Studies/Smart_Citizen/README.md) describes maintenance, and its
[own publication manifest](Studies/Smart_Citizen/MANUSCRIPT_MANIFEST.json) records
the edition, evidence standing and fingerprints separately from the 14 imported
originals.

This edition develops the rationale, research questions, source assessment,
mapping and evaluation methods, conceptual figures, explained JSON/SensorML/SWE
listings, and CMOS 18 author-date citations and references. The proposed sequence
is public source → adapter → CSAPI → Glaux, with captured replay and matched
interface comparisons supporting evaluation. Owned hardware is a later means of
filling physical-context gaps.

Version 0.5 adds the experimental Glaux client as a documented study subsystem, with its pinned standards-access dependency, explicit data/profile/presentation boundary, current public deployment, and unimplemented Smart Citizen traversal. Version 0.4 adds a dedicated experimental-server section and server-lineage figure. It identifies the tested 52°North/pygeoapi revision, Oracle endpoint, image digest, deployment adaptations, bounded comparison rationale, and unresolved backend/persistence configuration. Version 0.3 added an early illustrated environmental-monitoring system overview.
The authentic Smart Citizen Kit 2.3 photograph is a model reference, not verified
imagery of the installation represented by public registration 19639. The paper
distinguishes that immediate public-data case from later owned hardware and the
independent AirGradient comparison.

The retained seven-layer mapping explanation matrix is illustrated by the temperature
`SC-TEMP-01` and battery `SC-BATT-01` cases. These explain proposed interpretations
and their limits; they are not executed mappings or conformance results. The
publication manifest records the current edition's inventory and verification.

Every mapping must explain its CSAPI resource and SensorML/SWE carrier, underlying
DIM concept, exact MPSS rule, and project-authored choices. Each individual donor
concept must have a citation, edition and exact locator, with its role stated as
direct reuse, adaptation, interpretive constraint, or unestablished equivalence.
Paper narrative paragraphs, including the abstract and explanatory captions,
must contain three to five sentences. Headings, tables, code, and bibliographic
entries remain structural, as detailed in the paper guide.

It is a protocol and preliminary assessment: the adapter, run, CSAPI publication,
and screenshot comparison have not been executed, and no conformance result is
claimed. Maintain the paper as evidence is produced; its pending results and
screenshot sections must not be filled with invented outcomes.
