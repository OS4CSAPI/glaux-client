# Research

Destination for source assessments, standards comparisons, mapping studies, and
research registers. No research conclusions or source collection are supplied
by this organizational bootstrap.

Later material must be suitable and authorized for public release. Do not
bulk-import archives, manufacturer manuals, or private workspace material.
Distinguish source evidence, interpretation, open questions, and proposals;
research alone does not approve profile or design changes.
