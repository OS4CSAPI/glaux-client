# Research

The [Artifact Register](../ARTIFACT_REGISTER.md) is the authoritative index of the
published project research artifacts. Their source versions and dates are preserved;
the 24 October import is not a new research increment.

## Current research collection

- [Bibliography and source register](Bibliography/): the v0.10 narrative synthesis
  and structured source/evidence registers from 22 October 2026.
- [Research reports](Reports/): Resource Details research paper v0.15, Power Source
  recommendation v0.1, OSCAR worked mapping v1.0, and its short result brief.
- [Client source index](SOURCE_INDEX.md): exact sources, locators, fingerprints,
  and limits used by the implemented Details View and power mappings.

The master source register's source, gap, relationship, and disposition identifiers
remain unchanged. The repository artifact keys have a separate purpose. Research
findings and recommendations retain their recorded standing; importing a report
neither adopts its unresolved proposals nor establishes a new conformance result.

[MERLIN continuity](../Plans/CONTINUITY.md) records completed work and remaining
research/design decisions. Supporting archival wrappers, preserved old release
bundles, standalone manufacturer manuals, and unrelated workspace material are not
part of this selected publication.
