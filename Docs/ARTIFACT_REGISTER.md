## Provenance and byte integrity

[ARTIFACTS.json](ARTIFACTS.json) records each artifact's stable repository key,
role, repository-relative path, release version and date, original source location,
source SHA-256, copied-file SHA-256, byte length, import date and standing. The two
hashes were calculated independently before and after copying and must match.
For the nine files selected from the complete OSCAR research delivery, the local
originals were also compared byte-for-byte with their exact ZIP members before
import. Provenance uses original filenames and archive-member paths.

The `glaux-artifact-` keys identify repository artifacts across future revisions.
They are separate from the source documents' bibliography/register identifiers,
MPSS relationship identifiers, and standard definitions. Those source identifiers
retain their own meanings and remain unchanged inside the original files.

The connected-example ZIP is itself one registered artifact. The selected current
files from the larger OSCAR research delivery are registered individually. Their
original references to other documents remain part of the preserved evidence;
this fourteen-file set is the scope of the import.
